# Salesforce reports Q2 Agentforce ARR and work-unit acceleration

Salesforce's second-quarter fiscal 2027 release reports Agentforce ARR above $1.5 billion, up more than 240% year over year, compared with $1.2 billion and 205% growth in the prior quarter. It also reports 7.0 billion Agentic Work Units delivered to date across Agentforce and Slack, including 3.2 billion in Q2, with quarterly volume up 97%; premium Agentforce One Edition and Agentforce for Apps bookings more than doubled quarter over quarter.

The update is commercially material, but the metric boundary changed: beginning in Q2, Agentforce ARR includes Slackbot and Headless 360. Salesforce defines ARR as the annualized recurring value of active subscription agreements and AWUs as discrete production tasks executed by AI agents. The release does not quantify how much of the ARR increase came from the expanded definition.

## Commercial evidence gate

This qualifies as a monetization green shoot and updates the May evidence.

- Company: Salesforce.
- Payer: enterprise customers with active Agentforce and related AI subscription agreements; customer count and concentration are not disclosed.
- Toll-gate mechanism: recurring subscriptions and premium Sales and Service SKUs containing agentic capabilities.
- Revenue bridge: Agentforce ARR above $1.5 billion, up more than 240% year over year; premium-SKU bookings more than doubled quarter over quarter.
- Measured period: quarter ended July 31, 2026; ARR is measured at period end, bookings are quarter-over-quarter, and 3.2 billion AWUs were delivered during Q2.
- Baseline and repeatability: the May release reported $1.2 billion Agentforce ARR and 3.8 billion cumulative AWUs. Q2 adds another quarterly observation and an in-quarter production-task denominator, although the ARR definition changed.
- Attribution quality: high for the primary earnings disclosure and production-work definition; medium for sequential ARR interpretation because Slackbot and Headless 360 were newly included.
- Still unproven: like-for-like Agentforce ARR growth, recognized agent revenue, customer count and concentration, retention, renewal, gross margin, serving and correction costs, and customer-level outcomes.
- Strongest falsifier: a like-for-like restatement showing little organic Agentforce growth, or later disclosure of weak renewal, usage contraction, or poor agent-specific margins.
- Next verification trigger: Q3 disclosure separating legacy Agentforce, Slackbot, and Headless 360 ARR, plus customer, renewal, recognized-revenue, and unit-cost measures.
