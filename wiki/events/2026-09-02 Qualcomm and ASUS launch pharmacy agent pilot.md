# Qualcomm and ASUS launch pharmacy agent pilot

Qualcomm and ASUS announced an on-device pharmaceutical AI agent for medication review, drug-interaction and duplicate-therapy checks, and pharmacist-supported patient education. More than 50 demonstration pharmacies across southern Taiwan are to receive donated AI PCs and edge devices; Evergreen Pharmacy and Yes Chain Pharmacy are named among planned deployments.

The workflow keeps a pharmacist in control and runs inference locally using an optimized 20-billion-parameter model and Taiwan Food and Drug Administration data. It is a bounded regulated-workflow pilot, not autonomous clinical decision-making or commercial adoption evidence.

## Commercial evidence gate

- **Payer:** none identified; devices are donated under the program.
- **Toll-gate mechanism:** potential future edge hardware, deployment, model-optimization, and support sales, but none is disclosed.
- **Evidence boundary:** 50-plus demonstration sites and named planned deployments establish pilot reach, not paid deployment, repeat usage, outcomes, or revenue.
- **Next verification trigger:** live-site count, medication-review volume, measured error or time improvement, pharmacist acceptance, paid expansion, renewal, or attributable hardware/service revenue.
