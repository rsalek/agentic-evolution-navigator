# Amber International reports agentic revenue and margin expansion

Amber International reported $7.357 million of “Agentic Revenue” for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, up 62.2% year over year and 69.6% quarter over quarter. This represented 52.8% of its $13.921 million total quarterly revenue. The category comprised $3.5 million from newly recognized A-MM Agentic Market Making revenue and $3.8 million from Marketing and Enterprise Solutions.

The company reported total gross profit of $11.064 million and a 79.5% gross margin, up from 67.7% in the prior quarter. It attributed the margin improvement partly to the new, higher-margin A-MM contribution and returned to positive operating income and adjusted EBITDA. MIA, its marketing agent, reportedly runs a substantial share of daily campaign operations for more than 100 enterprise customers within the company's wholly owned marketing businesses.

## Commercial evidence gate

- **Payer:** token projects and market-making clients for A-MM; enterprise marketing customers for the bundled Marketing and Enterprise Solutions category.
- **Toll-gate mechanism:** integrated technology, platform, operational, risk-reporting, and market-making service agreements; management previously described expected middle- to long-term agreements that expand with data and parameter requirements.
- **Metric and period:** $7.357 million agentic-category revenue in Q2 2026, including $3.5 million of first-quarter A-MM recognition; category revenue rose 62.2% year over year and 69.6% quarter over quarter.
- **Baseline and denominator:** $4.536 million in Q2 2025 and $4.337 million in Q1 2026 after comparative periods were conformed to the newly introduced category; Q2 agentic revenue was 52.8% of total revenue.
- **Repeatability:** the broader category has conformed comparative periods and more than 100 enterprise marketing customers; A-MM has only one quarter of recognized revenue, and renewal is not reported.
- **Attribution quality:** high for filed revenue amounts and A-MM's $3.5 million contribution; medium for the wider “agentic” category because it newly relabels pre-existing Marketing and Enterprise Solutions revenue and does not isolate MIA revenue or segment gross margin.
- **Still unproven:** A-MM customer count, concentration, pricing, backlog, realized contract duration, renewal, revenue share or fee mechanics, MIA-specific revenue, segment margin, cash conversion, and whether Ambre monetizes.
- **Strongest falsifier:** subsequent disclosure shows A-MM revenue was concentrated, non-recurring, or market-cycle-driven, while most “agentic” revenue reflects reclassified legacy marketing services rather than agent-generated economics.
- **Next verification trigger:** the planned Investor Day and next filing, especially A-MM customers, backlog, repeat revenue, fee structure, segment margin, MIA revenue, and retained-agent usage.

This is an emerging monetization green shoot with direct agent-product revenue and a margin bridge, but not yet measured recurring agent economics.
