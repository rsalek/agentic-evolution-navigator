# Coinbase reports x402 concentration in on-chain agentic finance

Coinbase's Q2 2026 earnings release states that more than 97% of on-chain agentic transactions used Coinbase's x402 protocol during Q2 2026. It also reports that more than 99% of on-chain agentic commerce used USDC and more than 90% of agentic stablecoin transaction volume ran on Base. Coinbase's x402 facilitator documentation publishes a free allowance of 1,000 transactions per month followed by a $0.001-per-transaction charge.

## Why it matters

This is the first accepted Coinbase evidence that connects repeated agentic transaction activity to a published infrastructure toll gate. It is an emerging monetization green shoot at the product and protocol-infrastructure level, not evidence of x402 revenue recognized by Coinbase.

## Commercial evidence gate

- Payer: an agent, agent operator, or service buyer funds the underlying stablecoin transaction; Coinbase's facilitator is the proposed infrastructure toll gate.
- Toll-gate mechanism: Coinbase-hosted x402 verification and settlement, priced at $0.001 per transaction after the published free tier.
- Revenue bridge: explicit published facilitator pricing, but no x402 transaction revenue, take rate, or margin is separately disclosed in the Q2 release.
- Measured period: Q2 2026, quarter ended June 30, 2026.
- Repeatability: the release reports a quarter-level share of agentic transactions, but not absolute transaction count, dollar value, active agents, active payers, or active merchants.
- Attribution quality: high for Coinbase's reported x402 share within its defined on-chain agentic-finance category; low for realized Coinbase revenue because the release does not bridge protocol activity to financial statements.
- Still unproven: absolute x402 volume and value, repeat-agent and merchant breadth, facilitator conversion, revenue, retention, dispute and failure rates, and agent-attributed margin.
