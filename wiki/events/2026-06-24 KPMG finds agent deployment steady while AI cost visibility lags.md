# KPMG finds agent deployment steady while AI cost visibility lags

KPMG's Q2 2026 U.S. AI Quarterly Pulse Survey reports agent deployment at 53%, broadly steady versus 55% in the prior quarter, while the share of organizations orchestrating multiple agents across workflows doubled from 9% to 18%. It reports that 48% use agents to automate cross-functional workflows. At the same time, only 26% report full, real-time visibility into AI operating costs, 35% identify AI cost management and economic literacy as a barrier, and only 36% have direct token or usage controls. The survey captured 204 U.S. C-suite and business leaders from organizations with at least $1 billion in annual revenue.

This is primary survey evidence of broad enterprise coordination and an economics-control bottleneck, not direct operator telemetry or proof of safe action-level production scale. It challenges a simple rollback narrative while preserving a material falsifier for agent adoption: deployment without cost visibility may not produce durable value.
