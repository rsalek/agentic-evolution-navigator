# Fortinet expands FortiGate and FortiSASE for AI-driven traffic

Fortinet announced the FortiGate 1200G series with FortiSASE Outpost, combining local network enforcement with cloud-delivered SASE services under shared FortiOS policy, threat intelligence, security context, monitoring, and zero-trust decisions. The company positions the product for hybrid environments, AI infrastructure, encrypted traffic, sovereignty, and compliance requirements. It says agents, applications, and connected devices are generating growing east-west traffic that can be inspected locally to reduce latency and keep sensitive processing within defined boundaries.

Fortinet's staged release reports vendor benchmark figures including 397 Gbps firewall throughput, 40 Gbps threat protection, 40 million concurrent sessions, and one million connections per second. The comparison table notes that competitor measurements use public data and may differ in methodology. FortiGate 1200G is expected to be available in Q3 2026.
