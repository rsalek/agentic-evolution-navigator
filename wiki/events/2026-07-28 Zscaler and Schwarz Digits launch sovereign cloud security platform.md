# Zscaler and Schwarz Digits launch sovereign cloud security platform

Zscaler and Schwarz Digits announced a partnership operating the Zscaler Zero Trust Exchange on Schwarz Digits' STACKIT cloud infrastructure in Germany. The joint sovereign Zero Trust SASE offering is described as available to customers across Europe, hosted in German data centres, and operated by STACKIT. It is aimed especially at public administration, defence, financial services, and healthcare, with identity verification, EU data residency, deployment, management, operation, and support included in the offering.

The primary release frames AI as a driver of malicious cyber threats and regulatory urgency under NIS2, DORA, and the EU AI Act. It also states that customer adoption and market acceptance remain forward-looking uncertainties. No named customer, deployment count, traffic volume, incident outcome, pricing, revenue, or agent-specific telemetry is disclosed.

## Why it matters

This adds sovereign-cloud distribution and identity-control readiness to the agent-security graph. It shows how security infrastructure can be placed inside a regulated regional operating boundary, but the partnership and availability statement are not evidence of customer adoption or monetization.

## Commercial evidence gate

This is not a monetization green shoot.

- Payer and toll-gate mechanism: not disclosed beyond the joint managed security offering.
- Revenue bridge: none; no contract, pricing, ARR, backlog, take rate, renewal, or customer expansion is reported.
- Measured period: launch date is July 28, 2026; no operating period or usage metric is provided.
- Repeatability and attribution: availability across Europe is an enabled capacity claim, not repeated customer activity or agent-attributed economics.
- Still unproven: named production customers, usage, adoption, threat reduction, margin, retention, and any agent-specific security demand.
