# Ant International launches Agentic Mobile Protocol

Ant International announced the open-sourced Agentic Mobile Protocol for wallets, super apps, wearable devices, and other mobile interfaces. The protocol includes delegated payment authority, agent identity and trust rating, money-back protection, and agent-to-agent settlement for nano-transactions. Ant International said Alipay+ connected more than 40 wallet partners covering 1.8 billion user accounts and 150 million merchants, while card-based agent-payment pilots with Visa and Mastercard were underway.

## Why it matters

The release adds a mobile and wallet control path for agentic payments with an explicit identity, authorization, settlement, and protection architecture. It is ecosystem and pilot readiness evidence; the partner-network totals are not evidence of AMP transaction adoption or Ant-specific agent revenue.

## Commercial evidence gate

- Payer: prospective users, agents, wallets, and merchants; no paid AMP deployment is identified.
- Toll-gate mechanism: wallet and payment processing, delegated authorization, A2A settlement, and trust controls; fees and take rates are undisclosed.
- Revenue bridge: existing Ant/Alipay payment infrastructure is the likely bridge, but no agent-specific revenue or economics are reported.
- Measured period: announcement dated April 28, 2026; partner-network totals are point-in-time ecosystem claims.
- Repeatability: no AMP transaction count, value, active agents, active merchants, retention, or dispute rate.
- Attribution quality: high for announced protocol capability, absent for measured economics.
