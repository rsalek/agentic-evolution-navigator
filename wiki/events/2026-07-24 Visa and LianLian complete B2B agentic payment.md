# Visa and LianLian complete B2B agentic payment

Electronic Payments International reports that LianLian completed Greater China's first live B2B agentic transaction using its LoopXPay agent, with Visa's Agentic Directory supporting trusted agent interactions. In the described workflow, the agent identified a product-sample requirement, recommended and compared suppliers, placed the order, and executed payment while operating under predefined spending controls and approval parameters.

The article says LoopXPay is registered in Visa's Agentic Directory and supports Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol. The public account is detailed but secondary; neither Visa nor LianLian's original joint release was located for this run.

## Why it matters

This is a second reported, named live payment workflow involving Visa after the BBVA transaction. Its multi-step procurement-to-payment sequence and stated control boundary strengthen the production direction of agentic payments, but one transaction does not establish repeat usage, merchant breadth, payment value, dispute handling, or durable economics.
