# Google publishes ATLAS v1

Google published the first AI & Economy ATLAS using 15 million aggregated and de-identified interactions across the Gemini App, AI Mode, and Gemini API. The release spans more than 150 countries, 140 languages, more than 800 occupations, and 4,000 tasks.

## What it establishes

AI use on the observed Google surfaces is broad across occupations but selective within them. Google reports a median task saturation of about 21% among occupations where at least one task crossed its usage threshold. Work-related tasks are 13.5% of the relevant Gemini App and AI Mode interactions.

## Evidence boundaries

This is a research-publication event, not adoption evidence for a specific agent workflow. The paper's less-than-10% end-to-end automation figure applies only to conversations classified as non-routine cognitive work, not to all workplace interactions. ATLAS excludes paid enterprise and Cloud API usage and major Google surfaces including Workspace, AI Overviews, Translate, Maps, Gemini Enterprise, and frontier agentic coding. Google says corporate integrations and agentic-workflow impact cannot yet be assessed.
