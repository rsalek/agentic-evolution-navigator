# Salesforce reports agentic search becomes first commerce journey step

Salesforce's Fourth Edition State of Commerce report says the use of agentic search as the first step in a shopping journey grew 200% year over year. Salesforce's analysis of platform shopping behaviour covers more than 1.5 billion global shoppers across 37 countries from Q1 2024 through Q1 2026; traffic referred from AI chats grew between 150% and 428% year over year in every measured quarter, while overall traffic grew in the single to low-double digits.

The report combines Salesforce platform behaviour with surveys of 3,450 commerce professionals and 4,689 consumers. It says 86% of commerce leaders expect large language models to be essential to product discovery within the next year, 28% of commerce organisations use agentic AI today, and another 44% plan to adopt it within six months. The article's opening summary rounds the consumer survey to 4,690, while its methodology states 4,689; that small internal discrepancy is retained as a measurement caveat.

## Why it matters

This closes a graph gap between identifiable agent-originated traffic and commerce discovery. It is scaled, repeated traffic telemetry and a directional signal that product discovery is moving into AI assistants and other channels outside brand-controlled properties. It does not establish completed purchases, merchant economics, or Salesforce's capture of a commerce toll gate.

## Commercial evidence gate

This is not a monetization green shoot for Salesforce.

- Payer: not disclosed.
- Toll-gate mechanism: no paid access, transaction fee, commerce attach, or separately priced agent service is identified.
- Revenue bridge: none; the report provides traffic, survey, and adoption measures, not Salesforce ARR, ACV, backlog, take rate, agent-attributed revenue, or margin.
- Measured period: platform behaviour is measured from Q1 2024 through Q1 2026; the report was published July 28, 2026; referral growth is reported year over year by quarter.
- Repeatability: strong for the reported Salesforce-visible traffic surface, but not independently reproduced and not a measure of repeat purchasing or paid agent activity.
- Attribution quality: medium for directional traffic growth because Salesforce reports its own platform analysis; low for Salesforce commercial outcomes because no conversion, order value, product attach, or revenue bridge is provided.
- Still unproven: AI-referred conversion, order value, repeat purchase, merchant coverage, serving cost, Salesforce Commerce Cloud or Agentforce attach, pricing, retention, and agent-attributed revenue.
