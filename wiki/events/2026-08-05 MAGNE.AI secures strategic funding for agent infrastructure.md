# MAGNE.AI secures strategic funding for agent infrastructure

MAGNE.AI, a private Web3-oriented AI hardware and infrastructure company, announced an additional US$2.64 million strategic financing round after a previously announced US$10 million round. The company says the new capital will fund engineering validation and commercial delivery of MAGNE AI BOX, integration with MAGNE L1 and MHash L2, and further development of MAGNE Agent Pay for x402-compatible agent, application, and digital-service transactions.

MAGNE.AI's product pages report local runtime verification for an x402-compatible Agent Pay flow, facilitator verification, and AI Task Receipt creation; the company also says MHash L2 testnet deployment remains pending. These are product-readiness signals. The funding announcement and product pages disclose no paying customer count, shipped-device volume, active agents, repeated transaction count/value, agent-attributed revenue, take rate, renewal, margin, or secondary-market access.
