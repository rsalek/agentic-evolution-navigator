# HP describes hybrid on-device agent architecture

In the replacement Six Five Summit transcript, HP executives described a hybrid agent architecture combining on-device models, cloud inference, employee-experience telemetry, endpoint security, and an IT control plane. They argued that local inference can reduce cost and protect sensitive data while cloud models handle tasks that exceed device capability.

The transcript gives several quantitative claims: 72% of organizations are deploying or piloting agents, an anonymous customer's AI cost rose from $20 million to $200–300 million, basic local inference could save $650,000 per 1,000 employees and potentially $2 million, and the agentic market could reach $6 trillion by 2028. No survey methodology, named customer, calculation, realised customer outcome, paid deployment, or agent-attributed HP revenue was located. Illustrations involving 300,000 devices and three million agents are scenarios, not observed adoption.

## Evidence assessment

- Workflow mechanism: route work between device and cloud under workplace telemetry, endpoint policy, and security controls.
- Adoption evidence: forecasts, an anonymous anecdote, and illustrative scenarios only.
- Commercial signal: none attributable to agent products; the transcript is product-positioning and architecture evidence.
- Open questions: source and denominator for the 72% figure, savings methodology, named deployments, realised usage and outcomes, attach, pricing, retention, and revenue.
