# CargoAi reports scaled production agent activity

A syndicated CargoAi operator disclosure says its AirAgent has been in production since October 2024 and read 446,000 shipment requests over two years. It reports 105,000 airline-customer emails handled in 2026 year to date, including 25,000 in October, and says seven in ten emails requesting rates, tracking, or booking are drafted or sent by the agent. It also reports 400,000 forwarder quotes built in 2026, 6,935 forwarding companies searching live rates in the last twelve months, 209 responding airlines, and tracking coverage across more than 260 airlines.

The disclosure names STRACKER and Flexport as users. STRACKER reportedly reduced selected quote preparation from hours to minutes while retaining human validation, and Flexport uses agents for freight consolidation and booking. CargoAi estimates 6,100 hours returned from airline email work and 87,000 hours from forwarder quoting, but these estimates apply fixed assumptions of five minutes per request, thirteen minutes per quote, and 160 work hours per person-month. They are not audited realized savings.

## Commercial evidence gate

This qualifies as an emerging monetization green shoot through repeated production activity, named commercial operators, and estimated operating benefits. Confidence remains medium because the disclosure is syndicated, paid status is not explicit, and the economics are modelled rather than audited.

- Company: CargoAi.
- Payer: airlines, general sales agents, and freight forwarders using CargoAi products; the disclosure does not identify paid contracts or payer counts.
- Toll-gate mechanism: the mailbox agent, QuoteAssist, CargoCONNECT APIs, MCP Connector, and access to live rates, booking, and tracking workflows.
- Revenue bridge: repeated production use and named operators create a plausible bridge to CargoAi's commercial workflow products, but no price, revenue, ARR, paid attach, renewal, or transaction fee is disclosed.
- Measured period: AirAgent production since October 2024; shipment requests over two years; airline emails and forwarder quotes in 2026 year to date; network breadth over the last twelve months.
- Baseline and repeatability: drafted answers reportedly rose ninefold in October versus September, and the multi-period request and quote counts demonstrate recurrence; accepted quote, booking, and paid-cohort denominators are absent.
- Maturity: scaled.
- Confidence: medium.
- Customer and market-share evidence: STRACKER and Flexport are named; 6,935 forwarding companies and 209 airlines indicate network breadth, not market share or paid-customer count.
- Still unproven: paid customer count, pricing, revenue, ARR, accepted or sent quote rate, booking conversion and value, realized labour reduction, retention, and margin.
- Strongest falsifier: high request and draft counts fail to convert into accepted quotes, bookings, paid contracts, retained usage, or realized cost reduction.
- Next verification trigger: primary company or customer disclosure of pricing, paid customers, agent revenue, accepted quotes, booking count and value, renewal, realized labour savings, and margin.
