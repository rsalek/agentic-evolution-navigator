# MongoDB and Cognition launch modernization workflow

MongoDB and Cognition made Devin for MongoDB Modernizations available to their customers. In early joint testing, the companies report that selected modernization tasks fell from five to six hours to just over one hour. Named customer BILT says Devin produced dozens of merged pull requests per day during a final modernization push.

## Commercial evidence gate

This is operating and maturity evidence, not a monetization green shoot. The disclosure gives no price, paid attach, contract value, agent-attributed revenue, renewal, margin, quality-adjusted outcome, or independently verified economic result.

The strongest falsifier is evidence that faster task completion produces unacceptable defects, review load, rework, or no durable customer expansion. The next verification trigger is paid customer attach, accepted-change and defect denominators, contract expansion, retained use, and customer-side cost or cycle-time measurement.
