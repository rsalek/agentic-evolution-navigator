# Fingerprint reports ARR, customer growth, and agent detection

Fingerprint reported more than $50 million of annual recurring revenue after nearly 200% revenue growth over two years, with its customer base growing 93% over the same period to more than 6,000 organizations across 56 countries. It also reported identifying more than one billion unique devices monthly and processing more than 80 million real-time API events each day.

The company launched Authorized AI Agent Detection in 2026. Its product documentation describes structured detection of authorized AI agents using Web Bot Auth, including provider, agent name, and identity-verification status. The announced ecosystem includes OpenAI, AWS AgentCore, Browserbase, Manus, and Anchor Browser.

## Commercial evidence gate

- **Payer:** fraud, security, commerce, and identity teams buying Fingerprint's device-intelligence and risk APIs.
- **Toll-gate mechanism:** recurring SaaS and API access for device, automation, risk, and verified-agent traffic classification.
- **Revenue bridge:** company-wide ARR above $50 million, nearly 200% two-year revenue growth, and 93% customer growth establish realized recurring monetization for the platform that now includes agent classification.
- **Measured period and denominator:** revenue and customer growth cover two years; the current base exceeds 6,000 organizations. Platform activity exceeds one billion identified devices monthly and 80 million API events daily.
- **Repeatability:** ARR, a multi-year growth period, a broad customer base, and daily API activity indicate repeated commercial use at the platform level.
- **Attribution quality:** medium. The recurring-revenue and usage evidence is primary company disclosure, but Fingerprint does not isolate Authorized AI Agent Detection revenue, paid attach, traffic share, customer count, or margin.
- **Still unproven:** agent-specific ARR, pricing, paid customers, attach, renewal, verified-agent event volume, detection accuracy, and incremental margin.
- **Strongest falsifier:** evidence that agent detection is free, lightly used, or bundled while almost all ARR is generated by conventional device and fraud products.
- **Next verification trigger:** disclosure of paid Authorized AI Agent Detection customers, verified-agent API volume, pricing or attach, renewal, agent-attributed ARR, or customer-confirmed outcomes.

This qualifies as an emerging monetization green shoot at the trust and traffic-classification layer, not proof of scaled agent-specific revenue.
