# Datasea begins agent marketing services for five customers

Datasea says its Chinese operating entities signed AI-agent digital-marketing cooperation agreements and pre-order arrangements with five customers. All five had begun receiving services by September 17, with approximately $1.10 million (RMB7.36 million) in aggregate services provided; named customer Beijing Judongjiujiu accounted for about $0.51 million (RMB3.45 million). The agent scope includes task orchestration, model invocation, content generation, ad placement, data processing, and marketing execution.

## Commercial evidence gate

- **Payer and toll gate:** the five enterprise marketing customers consume services under a recharge, consumption, and service billing model. Datasea provides agent-enabled campaign execution and platform support.
- **Metric, period, and baseline:** five service-started customers and approximately $1.10 million of services provided as of September 17, 2026. The release does not specify the service period, prior agent-customer count, billable usage, or cash received.
- **Revenue bridge:** anticipated fees are $0.45 million to $1.49 million per customer per month, but they are usage-dependent pre-orders. The $2.24 million to $7.45 million aggregate monthly range and $89.4 million twelve-month maximum are **not** minimum commitments, backlog, guaranteed or recognized revenue. Even the $1.10 million delivered service value awaits confirmation, settlement, payment, and accounting treatment.
- **Maturity and attribution:** production service delivery, not scaled recurring economics. Confidence is medium for the company-reported five starts and service value, low for repeatability and recognized agent-specific revenue. The package includes broader marketing, SaaS, and custom-development work, so its agent-only share is unknown.
- **Still unproven:** repeat monthly consumption, recognized revenue, collections, independent customer confirmation, agent-executed task count, gross margin, customer concentration beyond the named largest account, renewal, and marketing outcomes.
- **Strongest falsifier:** the service value is not collected or recognized, or subsequent filings show chiefly conventional advertising and customization rather than agent-executed work.
- **Next trigger:** the next SEC filing and customer disclosures showing recognized agent-service revenue, receivables and collections, usage by month, renewals, and segment margin against this initial five-customer cohort.

This is an emerging commercial green shoot for customer-side delivery and a usage-price mechanism, not evidence of $89.4 million ARR or repeat paid consumption.
