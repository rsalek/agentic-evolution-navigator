# Workday reports agent-customer and AI ACV growth

Workday's fiscal 2027 second-quarter earnings release reports that more than 5,500 customers use at least one Workday-built agent, up more than 35% from the prior quarter. The CEO also said AI drove more than 25% of new annual contract value in the quarter. Workday reported 11,500 organizations overall, implying that the stated agent-user count covers roughly 48% of the reported customer base, although customer overlap, depth of use, paid attach, and agent activity are not disclosed.

The customer count and sequential growth are directly agent-specific. The ACV measure is broader AI, so it should not be interpreted as agent-attributed ACV without a product bridge. This is stronger than launch or availability evidence but weaker than separately reported agent revenue.

## Commercial evidence gate

This qualifies as a monetization green shoot through scaled agent-customer growth, not proven agent-attributed revenue.

- Company: Workday.
- Payer: enterprise HR, finance, and IT customers with Workday subscriptions; the release does not say whether every agent-using customer pays incremental agent fees.
- Toll-gate mechanism: Workday subscription agreements and customer-expansion ACV inside its governed system-of-record platform.
- Revenue bridge: more than 25% of new ACV was attributed to AI, but Workday does not isolate the agent-derived portion. The direct agent evidence is 5,500-plus customers, up more than 35% quarter over quarter.
- Measured period: fiscal Q2 2027 ended July 31, 2026, with sequential customer growth from the prior quarter.
- Baseline and repeatability: the 35%-plus quarter-over-quarter comparison implies a prior-quarter base below roughly 4,100 customers; active-agent, task, renewal, and paid-attach denominators are absent.
- Attribution quality: high for the primary earnings disclosure and agent-customer count; medium for the commercial bridge because the ACV figure includes broader AI.
- Still unproven: agent-specific ACV or recognized revenue, pricing, paid attach, active agents, task volume, workflow outcomes, renewal, retention, concentration, and agent-specific margin.
- Strongest falsifier: evidence that most of the 5,500 customers merely enabled included agents without repeated use, incremental spend, renewal, or measurable outcomes.
- Next verification trigger: Workday's next quarterly disclosure of agent-specific ACV, paid attach, active-agent and task counts, expansion or renewal, and customer outcome measures.
