# CrowdStrike introduces Falcon Guardian for agent runtime security

CrowdStrike introduced Falcon Guardian, an AI Detection and Response product intended to discover known and shadow agents, connect prompt activity to endpoint actions, enforce runtime policy, and contain compromised agents across endpoint, cloud, and SaaS environments. The company says the underlying Falcon sensor is deployed across hundreds of millions of devices, providing distribution capacity rather than evidence of Guardian adoption.

## Commercial evidence gate

- **Payer:** existing or new enterprise Falcon security customers.
- **Toll-gate mechanism:** an agent-specific security product attached to the Falcon endpoint and managed-security platform.
- **Evidence boundary:** product availability and installed endpoint reach are readiness and distribution evidence only.
- **Still unproven:** Guardian pricing, paid customers, attach, active agents, detections, incidents, renewal, incremental ARR, and margin.
- **Strongest falsifier:** agent protection is bundled without incremental spend or customers prefer cloud, identity, or model-layer controls over endpoint enforcement.
- **Next verification trigger:** paid Guardian customer count, attach or pricing, active protected agents, incident volume, renewal, or agent-attributed ARR.
