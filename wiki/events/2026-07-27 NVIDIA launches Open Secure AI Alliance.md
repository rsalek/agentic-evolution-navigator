# NVIDIA launches Open Secure AI Alliance

NVIDIA announced the Open Secure AI Alliance with a broad group of technology, cybersecurity, cloud, enterprise-software, open-source, and AI-research participants. The stated goal is to build and share open tools for responsible AI use and agent security. The announcement describes contributions across identity, isolation, model scanning, secure coding, logs, evaluation, agent harnesses, and defensive AI operations.

NVIDIA linked the alliance to the recent Hugging Face security incident and said its open defense stack is intended to help defenders inspect, test, trace, audit, and govern agent systems across a multi-vendor ecosystem. The alliance launch is a coordination and readiness signal; it does not establish shipped production controls, adopter count, incident reduction, or paid security revenue.

## Why it matters

This closes a graph gap between the Hugging Face incident and the emerging trust-control response. It strengthens the security and governance direction while opening a counter-pressure to independent trust tollbooths if open identity, evaluation, and defense tooling becomes widely available.
