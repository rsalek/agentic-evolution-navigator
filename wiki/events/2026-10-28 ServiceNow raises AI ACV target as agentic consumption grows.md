# ServiceNow raises AI ACV target as agentic consumption grows

ServiceNow's third-quarter 2026 earnings release raised its full-year AI net-new annual contract value target to $1.7 billion from $1 billion at the start of the year and reported that year-to-date production agentic-AI consumption grew more than thirteenfold year over year. The target is forward-looking and covers ServiceNow AI more broadly than agentic products, but the same primary disclosure connects contracted AI demand with repeated production agent use.

The release also names Thrive as a customer using ServiceNow CRM and AI agents. Thrive reports 73% first-contact resolution, 63% lower time to resolution, and an estimated 14,500 engineer hours returned to customer-facing work. These are bundled workflow outcomes rather than clean agent-only economics. ServiceNow's one-percentage-point increase in its free-cash-flow margin target is likewise company-wide and is not attributed to agents.

## Commercial evidence gate

This qualifies as an emerging monetization green shoot through a higher contracted-AI target and repeated production agentic consumption, with medium agent-specific attribution.

- Company: ServiceNow.
- Payer: enterprise customers buying ServiceNow platform and AI subscriptions; paid agent-customer count is not disclosed.
- Toll-gate mechanism: recurring platform subscriptions, AI annual contract value, and governed production consumption inside enterprise workflows.
- Revenue bridge: the company raised its FY2026 AI net-new ACV target from $1 billion to $1.7 billion after AI net-new ACV exceeded expectations, while reporting more than thirteenfold year-over-year production agentic-AI consumption. AI ACV is broader than agent-specific ACV.
- Measured period: quarter ended September 30, 2026; consumption is year-to-date versus the prior-year period; the ACV figure is a full-year target.
- Baseline and repeatability: the prior $1 billion target and year-over-year production comparison provide baselines, but absolute consumption, paid deployment count, customer count, and cohort retention are absent.
- Maturity: scaled.
- Confidence: high for the primary metrics; medium for agent-specific commercial attribution.
- Customer and market-share evidence: Thrive is a named production customer with measured service outcomes; no agent-specific market share is disclosed.
- Still unproven: realized agent revenue, agent-specific ACV, absolute consumption, paid attach, active agents, renewal, customer concentration, correction cost, and margin.
- Strongest falsifier: evidence that production consumption is largely included or free, shallow, or concentrated and that broader AI ACV is not materially driven by agent products.
- Next verification trigger: agent-specific ACV or recognized revenue, paid deployment and active-agent counts, absolute task consumption, attach, renewal, and margin disclosure.
