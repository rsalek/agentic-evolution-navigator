# NCSC advises cautious adoption of agentic AI

The UK's National Cyber Security Centre summarized joint guidance encouraging organizations to start small with agentic AI, limit early use to low-risk tasks, apply established cyber-security controls, deploy incrementally through bounded pilots, and plan for failure. The guidance identifies broader access, unpredictable behaviour, faster-than-human actions, and difficulty explaining agent decisions as additional risks compared with non-agentic AI.

This is primary regulatory and security-readiness evidence. It strengthens the graph's control-boundary and bounded-service dynamics, but it does not establish commercial adoption, security-product revenue, or willingness to pay.
