# AIR exits stealth with agent-security customers and funding

AIR Security emerged from stealth with a context firewall for AI agents spanning agent discovery, continuous vetting of skills and MCP servers, policy, runtime protection, and a vetted add-on marketplace. The company confirmed Sequoia, Greenoaks, Swish, Netz, and TTP as investors; TechCrunch reported $50 million raised across two rounds.

In an operator interview, CEO Yair Saban said AIR had more than 20 customers, roughly one quarter of them large enterprises, with the strongest demand in financial services and pharmaceuticals. He also said the platform filtered about 27% of the online add-ons and skills it evaluated.

## Commercial evidence gate

- **Payer:** potentially enterprise security and governance teams, especially in regulated sectors; no payer is named.
- **Toll-gate mechanism:** cross-vendor discovery, continuous verification, policy enforcement, runtime protection, and a vetted marketplace.
- **Evidence:** more than 20 reported customers and observed demand indicate commercial engagement, but the source does not establish that they are paying, in production, or renewing.
- **Still unproven:** pricing, paid-customer count, ARR, ACV, contract duration, deployment stage, active agents, monitored add-ons, retention, gross margin, and named customer outcomes.
- **Strongest falsifier:** the reported customers are unpaid design partners or pilots and incumbents bundle equivalent controls into existing platforms.
- **Next verification trigger:** a named paid production customer, contract or pricing disclosure, ARR, renewal, or repeated monitored-agent/add-on volume.

Funding, product launch, and a static customer count do not pass the monetization-green-shoot gate.
