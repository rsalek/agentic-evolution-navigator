# IBEX reports AI-agent wins and implementations

IBEX reported two strategic AI-agent wins among nine new-logo wins in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 and more than 10 completed AI-agent implementations across five verticals to date. The company described these as successful implementations rather than pilots and tied the operating model to its Sierra partnership and Wave iX customer-experience solutions.

For the quarter ended June 30, 2026, revenue rose 11.6% year over year to $164.3 million. Full-year revenue rose 15.4% to $644.1 million. IBEX said quarterly growth included accelerating Wave iX growth and full-year growth included Wave iX and digital acquisition, but it did not disclose the amount attributable to AI agents. Fourth-quarter adjusted EBITDA margin fell from 13.9% to 12.3%, partly because of training costs for new client wins and delivery-centre transfers; full-year adjusted EBITDA margin was nearly flat at 12.8%.

## Commercial evidence gate

This is an emerging monetization green shoot based on customer wins and completed deployments, with a partial rather than isolated revenue bridge.

- **Payer:** enterprise customer-experience clients buying IBEX-operated services and Wave iX solutions.
- **Toll-gate mechanism:** contracted outsourced CX delivery and implementation of Sierra-powered and Wave iX agent workflows, integrated with human support operations.
- **Revenue bridge:** two fourth-quarter new-logo wins are explicitly agent-specific, and management says Wave iX growth contributed to quarterly and annual revenue growth. No agent-specific contract value, revenue, ARR, pricing, or attach is disclosed.
- **Measured period and baseline:** two of nine fourth-quarter new logos were strategic AI-agent wins; more than 10 AI-agent implementations had been delivered across five verticals by September 10, 2026. No prior implementation baseline is supplied.
- **Repeatability:** multiple implementations across five verticals and two new wins establish more than an isolated pilot, but cohort retention, expansion, recurring usage, and multi-period implementation growth are unreported.
- **Attribution quality:** medium. The new-logo count is agent-specific and primary; the revenue and margin figures cover the whole company, while Wave iX includes capabilities beyond autonomous agents.
- **Still unproven:** agent contract value, recognized revenue, pricing, paid attach, active-agent or interaction volume, implementation concentration, renewal, expansion, customer outcomes, and agent-specific margin.
- **Strongest falsifier:** evidence that the two wins are small, unpaid, or implementation-only and that the broader Wave iX growth comes mainly from non-agent services without durable renewal or expansion.
- **Next verification trigger:** named customer confirmation or disclosure of agent ACV, recognized revenue, paid deployment count, interaction volume, renewal or expansion, outcome savings, and contribution margin.
