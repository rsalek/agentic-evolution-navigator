# Marvell reports broad AI infrastructure demand

Marvell CEO Matt Murphy described custom accelerators, switching, optics, connectivity, and CXL memory expansion as infrastructure for training and inference. He said AI data-centre activity was running above $2 billion per quarter and that customer adoption of CXL memory expansion was accelerating. The latest filed quarter before the interview reported $2.418 billion of total revenue and $1.833 billion of data-centre revenue, or 76% of total revenue, with management citing broad AI demand and exceptional AI-related bookings.

The filed results validate strong, scaled AI-infrastructure demand but not the interview's newer above-$2-billion quarterly figure, which awaits the next results. More importantly, neither source isolates agentic workloads. Agent memory, inference, and connectivity are plausible demand mechanisms, not proven agent-attributed revenue.

## Evidence assessment

- Workflow mechanism: custom compute, memory expansion, switching, and optical interconnect support inference scale and context movement.
- Adoption evidence: scaled data-centre revenue and management-reported customer demand; agent workload share is unknown.
- Commercial signal: scaled AI-infrastructure demand, but unproven as agentic commercial traction because no agent-attributed payer or revenue bridge is disclosed.
- Open questions: next-quarter validation of the run rate, agentic share of workloads and bookings, customer concentration, attach, pricing, margin, and durability.
