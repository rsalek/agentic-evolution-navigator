# Datasea reports repeat service-value growth

Datasea says the aggregate value of services provided under its five previously disclosed AI-agent marketing arrangements reached approximately $4.0 million as of October 8, 2026. That is an increase of approximately $2.9 million, or 264%, from the $1.10 million disclosed on September 17 across the same five-customer cohort. The issuer attributes the increase to continued platform use and service consumption across content generation, creative production, ad placement, analytics, marketing optimization, SaaS applications, and related workflows.

## Commercial evidence gate

- **Payer and toll gate:** the same five enterprise customers consume services under recharge, consumption, and service-billing arrangements.
- **Metric, period, and baseline:** aggregate service value increased from approximately $1.10 million on September 17 to approximately $4.0 million on October 8, a $2.9 million or 264% increase over three weeks. Customer count remained five.
- **Repeatability:** this is the first follow-up period showing additional consumption from the original cohort. It is stronger than a single service-start disclosure, but it does not establish renewal or a durable multi-period growth rate.
- **Revenue bridge:** services are being assigned commercial value under usage-linked arrangements. Datasea still does not say that the $4.0 million was recognized as revenue, invoiced, collected, or included in backlog. The previously cited $89.4 million annualized ceiling remains nonbinding and is not used as evidence.
- **Attribution quality:** medium. The update is a primary issuer disclosure with a like-for-like cohort and baseline, but no customer confirmation or accounting reconciliation. The service bundle includes broader marketing, SaaS, and implementation activity, so the agent-only share remains unknown.
- **Still unproven:** recognized revenue, receivables and cash collection, monthly usage by customer, repeat agent-executed task volume, gross margin, renewal, customer outcomes, and independent confirmation.
- **Strongest falsifier:** a subsequent filing shows that the service value was not recognized or collected, was reversed, or primarily reflected conventional advertising and implementation rather than agent-executed work.
- **Next trigger:** Datasea's next SEC filing or customer disclosure reconciling the $4.0 million to recognized revenue, receivables, collections, continued usage, renewal, and segment margin.

This is an emerging monetization green shoot because the same customer cohort generated repeated, measured service value over a defined follow-up period. It is not evidence of $4.0 million in recognized agent revenue or a repeatable annual run rate.
