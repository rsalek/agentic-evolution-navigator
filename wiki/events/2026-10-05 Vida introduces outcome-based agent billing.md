# Vida introduces outcome-based agent billing

Vida Global introduced an outcome-based billing option for its AI agents alongside its existing usage-based model. A business defines a verifiable result—such as a qualified lead, warm transfer, completed onboarding, booked meeting, or recovered customer—and Vida bills when the agreed outcome occurs. The company says the model is initially available in performance marketing, insurance, and other lead-driven industries.

## Commercial evidence gate

- **Payer and toll gate:** a business customer pays Vida for a verified completed outcome. Vida's platform logs the outcome against customer-defined metrics and KPIs.
- **Pricing and revenue bridge:** the outcome-based model links a completed agent action directly to billing. Vida does not disclose the price per outcome. Its public pricing page separately says custom pilots start at $1,500 and are credited toward the first production month.
- **Measured period and baseline:** the model was announced on October 5, 2026. No billed-outcome count, value, customer baseline, conversion, or comparison with usage billing is disclosed.
- **Customer or market-share evidence:** Vida says it serves direct enterprises and a reseller, agency, and partner network, but does not identify an outcome-billed customer or quantify adoption.
- **Maturity and attribution:** announcement-stage pricing evidence. Confidence is high for the filed pricing mechanism and low for realized adoption, repeatability, and economics.
- **Still unproven:** outcome-billed customers, completed-outcome volume, average price, recognized revenue, attach, renewal, dispute and reversal rates, gross margin, and customer-level ROI.
- **Strongest falsifier:** customers retain usage billing or contest outcome definitions often enough that outcome billing produces little adoption, revenue, or margin.
- **Next trigger:** an SEC filing or customer disclosure with billed-outcome count and value, customer adoption, revenue mix, renewal, dispute rates, and contribution margin.

This is a pricing-led monetization green shoot under the graph's commercial gate, not evidence of realized outcome-based revenue. For public-equity triage, VIDA remains a watchlist candidate rather than an investment conclusion until adoption and economics are disclosed.
