# ServiceNow reports AI ACV and agentic deployment growth

ServiceNow's second-quarter 2026 earnings release reports that ServiceNow AI crossed $1 billion in annual contract value and that agentic deployments of ServiceNow AI increased ninefold over nine months. The same release reports $29.0 billion of total remaining performance obligations, $13.2 billion of current RPO, 123 transactions above $1 million in net-new ACV, and 658 customers above $5 million in ACV, but those broader measures are not attributed to agents.

This is repeated commercial and deployment evidence inside a system-of-record platform, not merely a launch. The direct revenue boundary remains incomplete: ServiceNow discloses AI ACV and agentic-deployment growth in the same primary earnings release but does not isolate agent-derived ACV, absolute agent deployments, paid attach, or customer count.

## Commercial evidence gate

This qualifies as an emerging monetization green shoot through contracted AI value plus repeated agentic-deployment growth, with mixed attribution.

- Company: ServiceNow.
- Payer: enterprise customers contracting for ServiceNow AI and workflow-platform subscriptions; agent-specific customer count is not disclosed.
- Toll-gate mechanism: recurring platform subscriptions and AI annual contract value embedded in governed enterprise workflows, including AI Control Tower and agentic applications.
- Revenue bridge: ServiceNow AI exceeded $1 billion of ACV in Q2 2026. The company does not disclose what share is attributable to agentic products or deployments.
- Measured period: quarter ended June 30, 2026; agentic deployments increased ninefold across the preceding nine months.
- Baseline and repeatability: the nine-month deployment comparison and contracted ACV establish repeated demand, but absolute deployment, customer, usage, renewal, and transaction denominators are absent.
- Attribution quality: high for the primary earnings metrics; medium for agent-specific monetization because the ACV boundary covers broader ServiceNow AI.
- Still unproven: agent-specific ACV and recognized revenue, absolute and paid deployments, active agents, task volume, attach, renewal, customer concentration, product margin, and outcome economics.
- Strongest falsifier: evidence that the ninefold deployment growth is mostly free, shallow, duplicated, or inactive and contributes little to renewal or incremental ACV.
- Next verification trigger: ServiceNow's next quarterly disclosure separating agentic ACV, paid deployment count, active-agent or task volume, attach, renewal, and AI gross-margin effects.
