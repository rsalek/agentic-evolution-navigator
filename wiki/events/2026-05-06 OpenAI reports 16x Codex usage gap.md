# OpenAI reports 16x Codex usage gap

OpenAI B2B Signals reported that firms at the 95th percentile of AI use sent 16x as many Codex messages per worker as firms at the 50th percentile. The same frontier firms generated 3.5x as many tokens per worker across OpenAI tools, up from 2x in April 2025.

## Why it matters

The largest reported gap appears in an agentic developer tool rather than in more familiar search, upload, or analysis features. This is material evidence that depth of agent use is separating the most intensive firms from the median.

## Evidence boundaries

The figures are relative provider-specific intensity ratios. They do not disclose absolute messages, workers, firms, completed tasks, accepted code, productivity, quality, or financial outcomes. OpenAI describes tokens as an imperfect proxy for business value. The evidence therefore establishes production use and dispersion, not absolute market scale or causal impact.
