# Fortinet launches FortiSOC with agentic AI

Fortinet announced FortiSOC, a cloud-delivered SaaS platform unifying SIEM, SOAR, identity threat detection, threat intelligence, case management, and agentic investigation and response. The product is described as operating under analyst oversight with one subscription and a unified console.

## Why it matters

This is a concrete security-control and system-of-record path for bounded agentic operations. It also provides the primary-company correction for a new candidate that claimed Fortinet had acquired Virtue AI: Fortinet's official announcement supports a FortiSOC launch, while no primary acquisition disclosure was found.

## Commercial evidence gate

- Payer and toll-gate mechanism: enterprise security customers are the prospective payer for a SaaS subscription; no price, paid deployment, or attach is disclosed.
- Revenue bridge: possible subscription and platform consolidation revenue, but no agent-attributed revenue or economic outcome is reported.
- Measured period: announcement dated June 16, 2026; no customer operating period or denominator.
- Repeatability and attribution: no active customer, alert, response, retention, margin, or agent-specific usage metrics.
