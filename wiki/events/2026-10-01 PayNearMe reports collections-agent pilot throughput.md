# PayNearMe reports collections-agent pilot throughput

PayNearMe reports that Indiana Finance Company piloted its AI Servicing and Collections Agent for proactive payment outreach and inbound customer interactions. In one outbound campaign, the agent placed as many calls in one hour as approximately 28 support staff members. Ninety percent of targeted accounts received a voicemail or Smart Link, completed a live conversation, or transferred to support staff.

The agent operates inside PayXM and can authenticate customers, provide account information, accept or schedule payments, capture promises to pay, send payment links, manage opt-outs, and hand off to staff. PayNearMe says the product is available to clients, but the named evidence remains a pilot campaign and the company does not report the campaign duration, account count, completed-payment rate, collected value, cost per outcome, error rate, or paid status.

## Commercial evidence gate

This is material pilot and operating-throughput evidence, not a monetization green shoot.

- Payer: Indiana Finance Company is the named operator, but PayNearMe does not say whether the pilot was paid or disclose any other paying deployment.
- Toll-gate mechanism: access to an agent embedded in the PayXM payment platform. Pricing, subscription or consumption terms, and contract value are undisclosed.
- Revenue bridge: the agent can complete payment actions inside PayXM, but no agent-attributed PayNearMe revenue, fee, attach, expansion, or transaction value is reported.
- Measured period: one outbound campaign. The date, duration, and number of targeted accounts are absent.
- Baseline or denominator: one agent hour is compared with the hourly call output of approximately 28 support staff; 90% of targeted accounts reached one of several contact or handoff states. The account denominator is absent.
- Repeatability: a named pilot establishes operation, but no multi-period campaign, renewal, repeated-payment, or retained-use series is disclosed.
- Attribution quality: medium because PayNearMe names the customer and quotes its COO, but the vendor authored the disclosure and provides no independent customer report or measurement protocol.
- Still unproven: paid deployment, customer growth, price, payment conversion, collections value, resolution quality, cost per successful payment, compliance outcomes, renewal, margin, and agent-attributed revenue.
- Strongest falsifier: higher call throughput does not improve completed payments or collected value after failed contacts, escalations, compliance work, and supervision costs are included.
- Next verification trigger: named paid customers, repeated campaign volume, completed-payment count and value, conversion against a human baseline, cost per successful collection, pricing, renewal, and PayNearMe-attributed revenue.
