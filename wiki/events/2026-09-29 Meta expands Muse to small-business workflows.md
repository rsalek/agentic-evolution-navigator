# Meta expands Muse to small-business workflows

Meta made Muse for Small Business available in limited testing with connectors to business systems including Asana, Intuit QuickBooks, Shopify, Slack, Stripe, and Zoom. Meta names Mulholland Grocery as an early user, but does not say whether it pays or report a measured operating result. Actions that publish, send, or spend require approval.

## Commercial evidence gate

This is an emerging pricing green shoot, not realized revenue or broad adoption.

- Payer: a small-business owner or operator purchasing additional Muse capacity.
- Customer or market-share evidence: one named early user; paid status, customer count, conversion, and market share are undisclosed.
- Toll-gate mechanism: Power costs $20 per month for 500 million Muse tokens per week and Maximum costs $100 per month for 3 billion tokens per week; both auto-renew monthly.
- Revenue or economic bridge: recurring subscriptions sell higher agent capacity for connected business workflows.
- Measured period and baseline: launch on September 29, 2026; no operating period, free-tier denominator, paid cohort, task volume, or outcome baseline is reported.
- Repeatability and attribution: the billing cycle and weekly capacity are repeatable and documented by Meta, but no repeat customer use or agent-attributed revenue is measured.
- Still unproven: paying small-business users, conversion, retention, tier mix, completed-task volume, business outcomes, commerce value, inference cost, and margin.
- Strongest falsifier: most business use remains free or trial-based and weak retention or high inference cost prevents economic expansion.
- Next verification trigger: paying-business count, conversion, retained weekly task volume, tier mix, revenue, and gross-margin disclosure.
