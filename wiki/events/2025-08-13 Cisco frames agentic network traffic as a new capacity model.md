# Cisco frames agentic network traffic as a new capacity model

In its Q4 FY25 earnings-call materials, Cisco described a traffic model in which agentic AI workloads keep network demand persistently high through ongoing interactions, above the burstier chatbot interaction model. The prepared remarks said the earnings presentation included a slide illustrating this model. The accompanying slide explicitly says the graphic is illustrative and is not based on customer network data.

## Evidence boundary

This is management framing and infrastructure-readiness evidence, not customer adoption, observed market traffic, or Cisco agent-attributed revenue. The later Cisco WAN report provides a controlled task benchmark and should be kept as a separate event rather than silently treating this earnings graphic as measured telemetry.
