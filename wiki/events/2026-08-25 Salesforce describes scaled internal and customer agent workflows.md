# Salesforce describes scaled internal and customer agent workflows

At the Six Five Summit, Salesforce executives described Agentforce as an interaction layer over enterprise data, application semantics, and workflow systems. Marc Benioff said the internal Casey service agent had resolved five million support issues and that most customers were still at the beginning of adoption. The interview also said a Dell supplier workflow reaches 20,000 suppliers daily.

These are company statements. Earlier Salesforce material substantiates production use but not the current units: a July 2025 release reported one million Casey conversations, about 45,000 weekly conversations, and roughly 85% resolution; separate Dell material describes approximately 19,000 or 20,000 **users** across more than 90 processes, not 20,000 suppliers. The summit wording therefore should not replace the better-defined prior metrics. The existing May earnings event remains the graph's measured Salesforce monetization evidence: $1.2 billion of Agentforce ARR and 3.8 billion production Agentic Work Units.

## Evidence assessment

- Workflow mechanism: enterprise context and application semantics feed agents that act through existing CRM, service, Slack, and workflow surfaces.
- Adoption evidence: company-reported scaled internal use, plus prior official evidence for Dell workflow users; the summit's five-million and supplier-unit claims are not independently validated.
- Commercial signal: no new summit revenue, pricing, retention, or margin evidence. Do not double-count the separately reported Agentforce ARR.
- Open questions: current Casey denominator and period; autonomous versus assisted resolutions; the correct Dell population; customer-level outcomes, retention, and cost per successful task.
