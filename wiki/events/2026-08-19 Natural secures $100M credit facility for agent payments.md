# Natural secures $100M credit facility for agent payments

Natural announced a credit facility of up to $100 million from Upper90 Capital Management to support its payments and credit products for AI agents. The company says the facility is intended to scale with payment volume and deployed credit, following more than $40 million in equity financing. Natural's credit product page currently labels agent credit “Soon” and describes lines of credit, underwriting by agent track record, and real-time utilization views.

This is a material private-market infrastructure signal because Natural is attempting to own the capital, identity, authorization, payment execution, risk, dispute, and compliance layers around agent transactions. It is financing and product-readiness evidence, not proof that the facility has been drawn, that agents are transacting repeatedly, or that Natural is generating agent-attributed revenue.

## Commercial evidence gate

This is private-company financing and readiness evidence, not a monetization green shoot.

- Payer: prospective businesses, agents, and transaction counterparties; no paying customer cohort is identified.
- Toll-gate mechanism: prospective payment, credit, settlement, risk, dispute, and compliance fees; no pricing, take rate, or transaction fee is disclosed.
- Revenue bridge: the facility supplies balance-sheet capacity, not revenue. Natural reports no ARR, contracted revenue, deployed-credit revenue, transaction volume/value, or margin.
- Measured period: financing announced August 19, 2026; no operating measurement period is provided.
- Repeatability: the facility is capacity evidence. Natural's credit page is marked “Soon,” and no repeated agent activity or drawdown metric is reported.
- Attribution quality: high for the financing announcement and medium for product readiness; none for realized agent economics.
- Private-market posture: Natural is private and reports more than $40 million of equity financing, but no current secondary access, valuation, or qualified-investor route was established.
