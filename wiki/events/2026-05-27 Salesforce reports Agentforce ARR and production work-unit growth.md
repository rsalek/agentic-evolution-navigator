# Salesforce reports Agentforce ARR and production work-unit growth

Salesforce's first-quarter fiscal 2027 results report nearly $3.4 billion of combined Agentforce and Data 360 ARR, up more than 200% year over year, including $1.2 billion of Agentforce ARR, up 205% year over year. The release also reports 3.8 billion Agentic Work Units delivered to date across Agentforce and Slack, growing 111% quarter over quarter; bookings from premium Agentforce SKUs grew nearly 60% year over year; and more than 50% of Agentforce and Data 360 bookings came from existing customers. Salesforce defines ARR as the annualized recurring value of active subscription agreements and an AWU as a discrete task executed by an AI agent in production, such as resolving a case, updating a record, or triggering a workflow.

This is direct company-reported agent-attributed recurring revenue with production-usage and existing-customer expansion signals. It is stronger than announcement or deployment-count evidence, but it remains self-reported and does not establish customer-level concentration, retention, or agent-specific margin.

## Commercial evidence gate

This qualifies as a monetization green shoot.

- Company: Salesforce.
- Payer: enterprise customers buying active Agentforce and related subscription agreements; the release does not name customer-level payers, but says more than 50% of combined Agentforce and Data 360 bookings came from existing customers and that Agentforce is helping tens of thousands of businesses.
- Toll-gate mechanism: recurring subscription and support revenue, including premium Agentforce One Edition and Agentforce for Apps SKUs anchored in Sales and Service.
- Revenue bridge: $1.2 billion Agentforce ARR, up 205% year over year; the broader $3.4 billion figure combines Agentforce, Data 360, and Informatica Cloud ARR.
- Measured period: first quarter fiscal 2027 ended April 30, 2026; ARR is measured at the reporting period, bookings growth is year over year, and AWUs are cumulative with quarter-over-quarter growth.
- Repeatability: ARR is recurring by definition, the year-over-year comparison provides a prior-period baseline, and AWUs are reported as production tasks; renewal, churn, and net retention are not disclosed.
- Attribution quality: high for the separately stated Agentforce ARR and production-AWU definitions in the primary earnings release; lower for combined AI/data ARR and total Salesforce revenue because those include broader products and acquisitions.
- Still unproven: gross and net retention, renewal rates, customer concentration, realized GAAP revenue versus ARR run rate, incremental versus bundled spend, agent-specific margin, serving cost, correction/recovery cost, and whether AWU growth translates into durable customer outcomes.
- Strongest falsifier: subsequent filings showing Agentforce ARR deceleration, weak renewal or expansion, materially lower realized revenue than the ARR run rate, or production work-unit growth without sustained paid usage or economics would weaken the conclusion.
- Next verification trigger: Salesforce's next quarterly results, especially Agentforce ARR, Agentforce Apps revenue growth, bookings from existing customers, production accounts, retention, and any agent-specific margin or cost disclosure.
