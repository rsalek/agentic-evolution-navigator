# Cognition crosses $1 billion annualized revenue run rate

Cognition reports that its annualized revenue run rate crossed $1 billion on September 25. The company reported $492 million on May 27 and almost $900 million on September 8. The September 8 update attributes the increase to customers expanding their adoption of Devin and publishes a monthly Agent Compute Unit usage chart. The May update reports more than tenfold enterprise-usage growth since the start of 2026.

The latest disclosure names GE Aerospace, Rivian, Rohlik, and Exa as customers that use Devin with engineering teams. Earlier 2026 disclosures also name NVIDIA, Citi, Mercedes-Benz, and Modal. The evidence links paid agentic software products to a company-level revenue measure across three checkpoints. Cognition does not separate Devin, Windsurf, model, service, or acquired-product revenue.

## Commercial evidence gate

This is a measured private-company monetization green shoot with high confidence in the company-reported figures and medium confidence in durable economics.

- Company: Cognition.
- Payer: enterprise engineering teams that buy Cognition's Devin and Windsurf products. The company names customers but does not disclose the paying-customer count, contract mix, or concentration.
- Toll-gate mechanism: paid access to Cognition's software-development products and consumption measured in Agent Compute Units.
- Revenue bridge: company-reported annualized revenue run rate increased from $492 million on May 27 to almost $900 million on September 8 and more than $1 billion on September 25.
- Measured period and baseline: the three company checkpoints span about four months. The latest run rate is more than twice the May baseline. The May disclosure also reports more than tenfold enterprise-usage growth since the start of 2026.
- Repeatability: revenue and usage increased across several checkpoints, and Cognition names customers using Devin in ongoing engineering work. The company does not publish customer cohorts, retained Agent Compute Unit consumption, renewal, or recognized revenue.
- Attribution quality: high for the direct company disclosure and the link to expanding Devin adoption. Product attribution is incomplete because the revenue measure covers Cognition as a whole.
- Still unproven: recognized revenue, customer count, product mix, contract duration, net retention, customer concentration, gross margin, inference cost, support cost, cash conversion, and independent customer confirmation of the revenue measure.
- Strongest falsifier: evidence that the run rate reflects short-lived consumption, acquisition accounting, concentrated customers, non-agent Windsurf revenue, weak renewal, or uneconomic inference and support subsidies.
- Next verification trigger: financial statements or investor material that disclose recognized revenue, Devin and Windsurf product mix, paying customers, net retention, contract value, gross margin, inference cost, and customer concentration.
