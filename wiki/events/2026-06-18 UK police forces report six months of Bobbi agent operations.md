# UK police forces report six months of Bobbi agent operations

Salesforce reports that Thames Valley Police and Hampshire and Isle of Wight Constabulary operated the Bobbi contact agent for six months after a 12-week pilot. Bobbi handled about 200 non-emergency conversations per day. The forces report that 45% of contacts were fully automated and users rated the service 4.6 out of 5.

The vendor story separately describes the workload as equivalent to 14,000 citizen contacts annually, a figure that does not reconcile with 200 conversations per day. It estimates 3,266 operational hours freed each year. It also says Bobbi identified at least one high-harm offence each day and routed an average of two daily cases involving violence against women and girls to human intervention.

## Commercial evidence gate

This is scaled operating-benefit evidence, not a monetization green shoot. The named public-sector users, six-month period, daily volume, automation rate, satisfaction score, and estimated staff time strengthen production evidence. The inconsistent workload figures lower confidence in the annualized measures. The source does not disclose the payer contract, Agentforce price, usage charge, recognized Salesforce revenue, renewal, fully loaded cost, error rate, later recovery, or independently audited savings.

The strongest falsifier is evidence that the 45% automation rate includes routing or conventional scripted flows, or that correction and supervision consume most of the estimated hours. The next verification trigger is customer-published methodology, contract value, correction and escalation outcomes, fully loaded cost per resolved contact, and renewal or expansion.
