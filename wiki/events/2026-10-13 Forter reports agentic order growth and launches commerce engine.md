# Forter reports agentic order growth and launches commerce engine

Forter's merchant-network telemetry shows repeated AI-agent purchases rather than a protocol demonstration. Across August 1 to October 7, 2026, agentic orders grew 24% from August to September on a like-for-like agent set, or 48% including Instinct and Grok Bot after their September launch. The week Meta's Muse appeared, agentic orders were 229% above the prior-month comparison. Forter later described agentic orders as four times their August level, reported an average agentic order value of $223, and said Muse bought at 74% of stores that received any agentic order. Forter also says its identity network links nearly 98% of agentic orders to the represented person and that its Agentic Commerce Engine is live.

## Commercial evidence gate

- **Payer and toll gate:** Forter's prospective payer is the merchant using its commercial identity, fraud-decisioning, and agent-checkout orchestration stack. The toll gate is identity linkage, risk decisioning, and merchant-controlled checkout on each order.
- **Metric, period, and baseline:** 24% August-to-September growth on a like-for-like agent set; 48% including two agents first detected in September; a fourfold increase since August by October 13; average order value of $223; and Muse penetration across 74% of stores receiving any agentic order. The observed window spans August 1 to October 7.
- **Repeatability:** multiple agent products placed orders over more than two months across Forter's merchant network. Forter does not publish absolute order count, merchant count, repeat-user cohorts, completed-versus-refunded share, or independent validation.
- **Revenue bridge:** the activity traverses Forter's commercial merchant network and is addressed by a live identity, fraud, and orchestration product. Forter does not disclose Agentic Commerce Engine pricing, paid attach, incremental contracts, take rate, agent-attributed revenue, renewal, or margin, so the bridge is indirect and bundled rather than measured.
- **Attribution quality:** medium. Forter is the direct decisioning operator and publishes its methodology window, but the classifier, network denominator, customer mix, and revenue attribution are not independently audited.
- **Still unproven:** absolute transaction count and value, active agents and merchants, approval and fraud outcomes, chargebacks, customer expansion, product pricing, incremental revenue, retention, and unit economics.
- **Strongest falsifier:** an absolute-denominator series shows that the growth came from a small launch cohort, low-quality or reversed orders, or activity that merchants did not pay Forter to classify or orchestrate.
- **Next trigger:** Forter-reported absolute agentic-order count and value, active merchants, retained agent cohorts, approval and chargeback outcomes, named paid deployments, pricing, attach, renewal, agent-attributed revenue, and margin.

This is an emerging monetization green shoot because repeated agent-originated order value is passing through a commercial identity and fraud-control surface. It does not establish incremental Forter revenue or a separately priced agentic product.
