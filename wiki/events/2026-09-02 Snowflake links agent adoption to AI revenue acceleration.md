# Snowflake links agent adoption to AI revenue acceleration

Snowflake reported $1.49 billion of Q2 FY2027 product revenue, up 37% year over year, for the quarter ended July 31, 2026. Management said the quarter included a meaningful step-up in AI revenue, that CoWork consumption was scaling and driving revenue, and that CoCo delivered another standout quarter. Asked to separate the drivers of growth acceleration, the CEO said the broad AI portfolio, including CoCo, CoWork, AI functions, and the newer Cortex AI Gateway, contributed approximately half.

Agent-product customer activity also expanded. CoCo surpassed 9,100 accounts after adding more than 2,000 net new accounts in the quarter; CoWork reached 5,800 accounts, up nearly 11% quarter over quarter. The CFO said CoCo-using accounts consume more of the core platform. Management also named 1Password and Indeed as CoCo or CoWork users and described increased user growth inside adopting accounts.

The bridge is commercially meaningful but not cleanly agent-only. Snowflake groups agent products with AI functions, document processing, machine learning, notebooks, and the AI Gateway when describing AI revenue. The filing does not disclose revenue, pricing, attach, renewal, or margin for CoCo, CoWork, or Gateway separately. Management also lowered non-GAAP product gross-margin guidance to 74%, citing a higher mix of fast-growing AI workloads with lower current contribution margins.

## Commercial evidence gate

This is an emerging monetization green shoot.

- Payer: enterprise Snowflake customers consuming first-party AI and agent products and the associated core data platform.
- Toll-gate mechanism: consumption-priced Snowflake platform usage, including CoCo and CoWork activity that also increases core-platform consumption; Gateway-specific pricing remains undisclosed.
- Revenue bridge: management reports a meaningful AI-revenue step-up, says CoWork is driving revenue, says CoCo accounts consume more core, and attributes approximately half of the quarter's growth acceleration to a broader AI portfolio that includes agent products.
- Measured period and baseline: Q2 FY2027 ended July 31, 2026; CoCo added more than 2,000 accounts in-quarter to over 9,100, while CoWork reached 5,800 accounts, up nearly 11% quarter over quarter.
- Repeatability: management describes multiple quarters of observed consumption behaviour, but does not provide a product-level revenue series.
- Attribution quality: medium for agent-specific monetization because the revenue bridge combines agent products with non-agent AI capabilities and core consumption.
- Still unproven: CoCo, CoWork, and Gateway revenue; paid attach; active-user depth; retention; revenue per account; agent-task volume; accepted outcomes; and product-level margin.
- Strongest falsifier: evidence that the reported AI-revenue acceleration comes mainly from non-agent AI functions or migrations, while CoCo and CoWork accounts use bundled features without incremental durable spend.
- Next verification trigger: agent-product revenue or consumption by product, paid-account growth, attach or renewal, cohort retention, and contribution-margin disclosure.
