# Atlassian prices AI agent resolutions and Rovo usage

Atlassian announced usage-based billing for Rovo credits and Customer Service Management AI-agent resolutions beginning December 3, 2026. A resolution is billable only when the agent completes a customer request without human escalation and an LLM judge determines that the conversation was resolved.

Customer Service Management will charge $1.00 from the first successful agent resolution, with annual prepaid-pack discounts reducing the rate to $0.85 at 200,000 or more monthly resolutions. Rovo usage beyond included paid-plan allowances will cost $0.01 per credit; basic agent interactions consume 10 credits, while advanced agents and enriched Teamwork Graph calls use variable amounts.

## Commercial evidence gate

- **Payer:** organizations on paid Atlassian cloud and Customer Service Management plans.
- **Toll-gate mechanism:** per-successful-resolution outcome billing, prepaid resolution packs, and usage credits for agent actions and graph context.
- **Metric and period:** billing begins December 3, 2026; $1.00 per successful resolution, discounted to $0.85 for 200,000-plus monthly prepaid resolutions, and $0.01 per excess Rovo credit.
- **Baseline or denominator:** billing starts with the first successful Customer Service Management resolution; no free resolution allowance is included. Rovo allowances depend on application, plan, and seats.
- **Repeatability:** monthly billing, prepaid packs, and metered overage create recurring transaction units, but no realized post-launch volume exists yet.
- **Attribution quality:** high for the directly agent-specific price and billing definition; low for realized demand or revenue because charging has not started.
- **Customer evidence:** Atlassian says most paid-cloud customers have built-in AI allowances and reports 13% faster resolution among customers using these capabilities, but provides no customer count, paid-resolution volume, or independent outcome confirmation.
- **Still unproven:** billable resolution volume, payer conversion, overage adoption, judge accuracy, disputes, renewal, agent-attributed revenue, gross margin, and customer retention.
- **Strongest falsifier:** customers disable overages or agent resolutions, successful resolutions remain low, or judge errors and escalations make outcome billing unattractive.
- **Next verification trigger:** the first reporting period after December 3, including billable resolutions, pack purchases, overage revenue, active customers, escalation and dispute rates, retention, and margin.

This is an emerging pricing green shoot, not realized monetization or adoption evidence.
