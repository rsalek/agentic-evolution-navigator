# ECB opens digital-euro agent-payments exploration

The European Central Bank invited market participants to explore digital-euro innovation, including AI agents and micropayments. Workshops are planned for the first and second quarters of 2027, followed by experimentation from January through June 2027 and proof-of-concept reports.

## Commercial evidence gate

This is institutional readiness evidence, not a monetization green shoot. There is no live agent payment, payer, merchant, active agent, transaction count or value, price, fee, take rate, revenue, dispute outcome, or unit economics.

The strongest falsifier is that experimentation does not progress into a usable scheme with merchant acceptance and repeated transactions. The next verification trigger is participant selection, a working proof of concept, repeated transaction telemetry, authorization and dispute controls, and a disclosed fee or economic model.
