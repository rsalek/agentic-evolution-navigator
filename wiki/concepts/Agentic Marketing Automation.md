# Agentic Marketing Automation

Marketing work executed by agents across customer data, campaign workflows, channels, and measurement systems.

## Observable signals

- Campaigns or journeys executed with bounded human intervention.
- Conversion lift, time-to-launch, content-review rate, and cost per outcome.
- Native access to shared customer records and enterprise workflows.

## Current graph evidence

- [[wiki/events/2026-09-03 Amber International reports agentic revenue and margin expansion|Amber International]] reports that MIA runs a substantial share of daily campaign operations for more than 100 enterprise customers inside its wholly owned marketing businesses. The combined Marketing and Enterprise Solutions category produced $3.8 million in Q2 2026, but MIA-specific revenue, task volume, customer outcomes, and margin are not isolated.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-17 Datasea begins agent marketing services for five customers|Datasea]] reports five customers receiving agent-marketing services and $1.10 million of service value provided by September 17. Its usage-price ranges are nonbinding; recognized revenue and agent-only attribution remain open.
