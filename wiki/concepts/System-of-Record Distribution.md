# System-of-Record Distribution

Adoption through incumbent platforms that already control enterprise data, permissions, workflows, users, and budget ownership.

## Observable signals

- Native integrations rather than separate agent destinations.
- Named enterprise deployments and enabled employee populations.
- Workflow completion inside CRM, contact-centre, ERP, ITSM, or productivity systems.

## Current graph evidence

- ServiceNow describes forward-deployed engineers agentifying named customer production environments, including large operating estates; the surrounding customer figures are not agent-attributed.
- Five9 connects healthcare expansion to Epic integration, and its first-party Epic Toolbox release corroborates native workflow integration; the academic health-system customer remains unnamed.
- ServiceNow's C Spire and Rossmann stories add named scaled workflows with agent-attributed routing, time, closure, accuracy, and labour metrics inside established service operations.
- R1's pilot connects the Sierra agent layer across electronic medical records, scheduling systems, and account databases; Singtel similarly distributes Shirley through an existing customer-service channel.
- VA's awarded Salesforce agreement is a large public-sector procurement signal for agent-capable workflows inside existing data, scheduling, contact-centre, and collaboration systems. The release does not show which agents are live or isolate AI-specific spend.
- Snowflake's Cortex AI Gateway announcement positions the AI Data Cloud as a control plane for agents reaching models, tools, MCP servers, and enterprise systems; the integrations are preview-stage and do not establish named live workflows or agent-attributed outcomes.
- Salesforce's Q1 FY27 release reports Agentforce ARR, production work units, premium-SKU bookings, and existing-customer booking expansion, directly tying an incumbent system-of-record platform to an agent revenue bridge.
- Haizhi Technology reports that 69% of existing Atlas Graph-solution customers converted to demand for Atlas Agent, with 29 Atlas Agent customers and direct product-line revenue. This shows a specialist enterprise data platform using its installed base as an agent-distribution channel.
- Okta's Agent SSO release places agent identity in the same Universal Directory, policy console, and integration network used for human identities. The stated 20,000-customer base is distribution capacity, not Agent SSO adoption; the core feature is bundled free while broader agent governance is a separate subscription.
- Salesforce, Snowflake, MongoDB, and Oracle converge on governed data, semantic context, permissions, and application state as the agent control surface. Snowflake's company-wide internal sales agent is the strongest new scaled operator statement, while Oracle remains readiness evidence and MongoDB's customer outcomes do not isolate the database's economic contribution.
- The remaining gap is action-level evidence showing which system writes are autonomous, which are deterministic, which require approval, and how permission failures are handled.
- Snowflake's Q2 FY2027 update links more than 2,000 net new CoCo accounts and nearly 11% quarter-over-quarter CoWork account growth to higher core consumption and a broad AI-revenue step-up. Agibank's six Agentforce agents and 11 bots add a named banking deployment handling 70,000 monthly inquiries through WhatsApp and CRM records; Salesforce does not disclose the customer's contract economics.
- Snowflake's Agent Observability announcement extends the data platform into agent traces, evaluations, cost, and business-outcome attribution. The product remains pre-preview and provides no production customer or commercial evidence.
- Major US banks reportedly use their control of authentication, customer accounts, transaction permissions, and service terms to block or restrict outside agents. This is direct evidence that the system of record can decide whether an agent reaches data or executes a financial action, while the unnamed-source reports do not quantify incidents or economics.
- Walmart's planned Agent Profiles and Gateway show the opposite policy choice within a retail system of record. Walmart will assign approved agents separate identities, permissions, spending limits, and data boundaries while retaining control of payment methods, order approval, returns, and access. The announcement does not establish launch, use, or economics.
- The Bank of England and FCA survey shows why system access remains bounded in finance: current or planned agent use was no higher than 9% for approvals, trades, payments, blocking payments, or changing limits and controls, even though 80% reported some agent deployment.
- Salesforce's Agent Fabric update extends the incumbent control plane across internal and third-party agents, APIs, gateways, policy, evaluations, and budget limits. More than 1.2 billion daily requests establishes platform activity, but not the number of agent customers, paid attach, or agent-specific revenue.
- ServiceNow's Q3 disclosure pairs a raised $1.7 billion FY2026 AI net-new ACV target with more than thirteenfold year-over-year production agentic-AI consumption and a named Thrive deployment. The platform bridge is direct, but agent-specific ACV and realized revenue remain undisclosed.
- CargoAi shows that a specialist vertical platform can also become an execution surface by joining live rates, quotes, booking, tracking, APIs, and agent interfaces. Its network and activity counts do not establish paid penetration or transaction economics.
- AWS AgentCore adds a cloud control-plane route with tens of thousands of customers and Q3 agent deployments twice the prior three quarters combined. The platform meters execution and controls but does not disclose agent-specific revenue, active workload volume, or retention.
- Supabase adds a specialist data-platform route. AI tools create more than 60% of new databases, the platform provisions more than one million databases each week, and the company reports fourfold annual revenue growth. The evidence supports agent-mediated distribution through an established data layer but does not isolate paid agent cohorts or agent-attributed revenue.
