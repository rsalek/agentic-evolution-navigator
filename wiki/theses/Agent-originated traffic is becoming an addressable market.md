# Agent-originated traffic is becoming an addressable market

Web infrastructure is beginning to distinguish legitimate agents from undifferentiated bots, creating a basis for access policy, attribution, paid APIs, transaction fees, and agent-specific service tiers.

## Supporting evidence

- [[wiki/events/2026-08-04 Cloudflare announces Wallets for agentic commerce|Cloudflare announces Wallets for agentic commerce]] adds a provider-side mechanism for agent identity, delegated spending, and paid API/content access, but not live traffic or revenue.

- [[wiki/events/2026-07-14 AWS recognizes legitimate AI-agent traffic]] provides an infrastructure mechanism for classification.
- [[wiki/events/2026-07-16 DataDome reports 45 percent Q2 agent-traffic growth]] provides an issuer-reported growth signal.
- [[wiki/events/2026-06-09 Cloudflare presents Web Bot Auth for autonomous commerce]] adds a cryptographic identity and authorization mechanism, but not live traffic or monetization evidence.
- [[wiki/events/2026-07-28 Salesforce reports agentic search becomes first commerce journey step]] adds primary vendor telemetry across more than 1.5 billion shoppers, with AI-chat referral traffic growing 150% to 428% year over year in each measured quarter and agentic search's first-step use growing 200% year over year.
- [[wiki/events/2026-08-07 Cloudflare reports scaled Precursor monitoring and hybrid agentic traffic|Cloudflare reports scaled Precursor monitoring and hybrid agentic traffic]] adds operator telemetry from 206 million security-evaluation events across 73,438 zones and direct observation of human-to-agent session shifts, while leaving the agent-specific denominator and economics open.
- [[wiki/events/2026-06-10 Fastly reports AI request growth and Skyfire agent-commerce integration|Fastly reports AI request growth and Skyfire agent-commerce integration]] adds a primary five-month traffic-growth comparison and an edge identity/payment path, while leaving the agent-only denominator, paid deployment, and economics open.
- [[wiki/events/2026-05-21 Cisco measures 450 percent more network traffic per agent task|Cisco measures 450 percent more network traffic per agent task]] adds a primary controlled-workflow measurement of agent-triggered network intensity and inference dependence, while leaving market-wide traffic share and willingness to pay open.
- [[wiki/events/2026-08-13 OtterlyAI prices Agent Analytics within paid monitoring plans|OtterlyAI prices Agent Analytics within paid monitoring plans]] adds a direct subscription and capacity mechanism for classifying and monitoring agent-originated traffic. It strengthens addressability at the observability layer, while connected-domain scale, repeat usage, customer outcomes, and revenue remain unproven.
- [[wiki/queries/Dageno-surfaced private agentic company watchlist|Dageno-surfaced private agentic company watchlist]] adds a private-company layer around agent-mediated discovery and delivery: Peec AI, Profound, Gauge, AthenaHQ, and Scrunch sell visibility, attribution, or AI-ready serving mechanisms. Their evidence supports addressability but remains distinct from agent-payment or agent-originated transaction revenue.

## Challenging evidence

- Traffic growth does not establish willingness to pay, incremental demand, or positive serving economics.
- Salesforce's commerce report does not disclose conversion, order value, repeat purchase, payer, pricing, or Salesforce agent-attributed revenue; it strengthens addressability but not monetization.
- Cloudflare's Precursor snapshot establishes monitoring scale and hybrid traffic behavior, but does not disclose agent traffic share, customer expansion, pricing, or agent-attributed revenue.
- Fastly's traffic category combines agents with crawlers, bots, API systems, and other machine interactions; its 30% AI-request growth and near-half machine-traffic statement therefore do not establish agent-specific demand or willingness to pay.
- Cisco's 450% result comes from one controlled task rather than customer network telemetry; it shows potential network intensity but does not establish broad agent adoption, incremental demand, conversion, or willingness to pay.
- OtterlyAI's paid event capacity establishes a toll gate but not realized willingness to pay; plan inclusion may be bundled, and the company discloses no paid usage, retention, or agent-attributed revenue.
- The Dageno-surfaced companies mostly measure or influence AI discovery rather than prove agent-mediated purchases. Peec AI's reported $4M+ ARR and customer growth are strong company-level signals, but agent-originated revenue remains unallocated; Profound's enterprise scale and Agent credits likewise lack agent-specific ARR and retention.

## What would change the thesis

- Verified conversion, attributed revenue, paid access, or repeat agent-customer metrics would strengthen it.
- Predominantly extractive traffic with weak conversion and rising serving costs would weaken it.
