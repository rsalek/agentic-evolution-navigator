# Incumbent platforms are the distribution channel

Enterprise agents are increasingly entering work through platforms that already hold data, permissions, workflows, and customer relationships. Native platform integrations may therefore distribute agents faster than standalone destinations.

## Supporting evidence

- [[wiki/events/2026-06-18 Rossmann scales agentic store support]] shows six live agents operating through ServiceNow across more than 200 store-support categories.
- [[wiki/events/2026-06-03 C Spire scales agent email triage]] adds named scaled workflow and outcome evidence inside ServiceNow.
- [[wiki/events/2026-03-04 Singtel scales Shirley across support and roaming transactions]] shows a specialist agent vendor distributed through an incumbent telecom's customer relationships and service channel.
- [[wiki/events/2026-05-18 R1 pilots agentic revenue-cycle calls]] shows a specialist agent layer executing through incumbent healthcare records, scheduling, and account systems, although only in early testing.
- [[wiki/events/2026-07-15 SuccessKPI brings WEM to Webex Contact Center]] embeds coordination into an established contact-centre platform.
- [[wiki/events/2026-07-15 Tenon expands on ServiceNow AI Platform]] embeds marketing automation into an enterprise workflow platform.
- [[wiki/events/2026-05-04 FIS and Anthropic develop financial-crimes agent]] places a named-bank pilot inside a regulated system-of-record provider, while leaving production outcomes unproven.
- [[wiki/events/2026-05-04 ServiceNow agentifies customer production workflows]] names customer production environments and operating scale.
- [[wiki/events/2025-07-31 Five9 expands healthcare AI-agent deployment]] links AI-agent expansion to Epic integration and a multi-year ARR signal.
- [[wiki/events/2026-02-19 Five9 scales healthcare AI-agent commitment]] adds a three-year healthcare commitment with ARR expansion and claimed cost savings, although the agent-attributed share remains undisclosed.
- [[wiki/events/2026-05-05 Microsoft reports 15x active-agent growth]] adds direct platform telemetry showing rapid growth in agent objects inside Microsoft 365 and SharePoint.
- [[wiki/events/2026-07-24 VA awards Salesforce Missionforce agreement]] adds a $1.6 billion-ceiling public-sector agreement that expands an incumbent platform's data, workflow, collaboration, and agent-capable footprint, but it remains announcement-stage evidence for agent deployment and spend.
- [[wiki/events/2026-05-27 Salesforce reports Agentforce ARR and production work-unit growth|Salesforce reports Agentforce ARR and production work-unit growth]] adds a product-specific ARR bridge, production task measure, premium-SKU booking growth, and existing-customer expansion inside an incumbent platform.
- [[wiki/events/2026-08-20 Serval announces Catalyst general availability|Serval's Catalyst general availability]] challenges the distribution thesis with a direct AI-native service-management alternative reporting broad adoption and a named customer workflow benefit, although it still uses existing ticket and procedure data and does not disclose independent customer economics.
- [[wiki/events/2026-08-24 Okta makes Agent SSO generally available|Okta makes Agent SSO generally available]] places agent identity inside the incumbent directory, policy console, and integration network used by more than 20,000 Okta customers. The installed-base figure is not Agent SSO adoption, but the free core feature and separate governance subscription define a distribution-to-attach path.
- [[wiki/queries/Six Five Summit 2026 agentic AI evidence comparison|The Six Five Summit comparison]] finds the same governed-context mechanism at Salesforce, Snowflake, MongoDB, and Oracle. Only Salesforce supplies a separately measured agent-revenue bridge; the others strengthen the distribution mechanism more than the monetization claim.
- [[wiki/events/2026-08-26 Salesforce reports Q2 Agentforce ARR and work-unit acceleration|Salesforce's Q2 update]] extends the evidence to more than $1.5 billion of Agentforce ARR and 3.2 billion in-quarter production work units, while a changed metric boundary limits sequential interpretation.
- [[wiki/events/2026-08-27 Workday reports agent-customer and AI ACV growth|Workday's Q2 update]] adds more than 5,500 customers using organic agents, up more than 35% quarter over quarter, inside a platform already holding HR, finance, and IT workflows.
- [[wiki/events/2026-07-30 Workday defines action-based Flex Credit pricing|Workday's Flex Credit model]] adds an annual subscription and an agent-action meter inside the same system of record. It links distribution to a repeatable toll, but Workday does not disclose paid attach, credit consumption, renewal, or agent-attributed revenue.
- [[wiki/events/2026-07-22 ServiceNow reports AI ACV and agentic deployment growth|ServiceNow's Q2 update]] adds more than $1 billion of AI ACV and ninefold agentic-deployment growth over nine months, strengthening the incumbent distribution mechanism while leaving agent-specific ACV unseparated.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-01 CrowdStrike introduces Falcon Guardian for agent runtime security|CrowdStrike's Falcon Guardian announcement]] extends agent discovery and runtime enforcement through an endpoint platform deployed across hundreds of millions of devices. The installed reach is distribution capacity, not Guardian adoption or revenue.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-01 Atlassian prices AI agent resolutions and Rovo usage|Atlassian's outcome and usage pricing]] places agent monetization inside Jira, Confluence, Service Collection, Customer Service Management, and Teamwork Graph context. The price is explicit, but billing starts in December and no realized agent revenue is disclosed.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-08 Meta launches priced Muse personal agent|Meta's Muse launch]] combines distribution through WhatsApp and Meta's consumer surfaces with reported $20 and $100 agent subscription tiers, connected-app permissions, and Stripe Link checkout. It is announcement-stage pricing without paid conversion or active-use evidence.
- [[wiki/events/2026-10-05 HUMAN measures tripling agent traffic and Muse dominance|HUMAN's September telemetry]] adds a post-launch distribution result: Muse reached 40.34% of measured monthly agent traffic and millions of daily requests by month end after entering Meta's consumer surfaces. The provider-specific surface and free-tier launch prevent a market-wide or paid-adoption conclusion.
- [[wiki/events/2026-10-12 Sensor Tower reports early Muse retention|Sensor Tower's reported Muse cohort]] adds repeat-use evidence after the launch: 73.3% day-one retention, more than 30% on day seven, and 22.5% on day fourteen, alongside a 2.182 million daily-active-user estimate. Meta's cross-promotion and free tier remain alternative explanations for the initial strength.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-14 TSA scales traveler-support agent with measured economics|TSA's Ace deployment]] uses Salesforce Government Cloud, Service Cloud, Public Sector Solutions, Shield, and an existing case-management foundation to distribute a scaled agent workflow. It adds measured customer-side economics but no Salesforce contract or agent-revenue disclosure.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-20 Amazon blocks Meta Muse from its store|Amazon's live block of Meta Muse]] shows the inverse of distribution access: an incumbent merchant platform can deny a third-party agent access to customers, account data, advertising surfaces, and checkout even when the agent has its own subscription and payment architecture.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-29 Meta expands Muse to small-business workflows|Meta's small-business Muse expansion]] combines incumbent consumer reach with connectors to accounting, commerce, collaboration, and payment systems, while retaining a direct monthly usage toll. The named early user is not evidence of paid adoption.
- [[wiki/events/2026-10-21 Meta reports scaled Muse use and shopping activity|Meta's Q3 Muse disclosure]] turns distribution into measurable activity: more than 10 million setups in roughly six weeks, about 25% daily use, and shopping by more than a quarter of weekly active users. Paid conversion and agent-attributed revenue remain undisclosed.
- [[wiki/events/2026-10-22 US banks block third-party agents from customer accounts|Reported US bank restrictions]] show the defensive side of incumbent distribution. Banks can use account control, authentication, transaction permissions, and service terms to determine whether an outside agent may read or act, although the reports do not quantify incidents, customers, or economics.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-29 MongoDB launches priced Atlas Agent Engine preview|MongoDB's Atlas Agent Engine preview]] can charge metered agent runtime and memory consumption against existing Atlas commitments. This is a direct incumbent attach path, but the named Paysafe deployment is still building toward production and no paid consumption is reported.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-29 OpenAI launches persistent dots agents|OpenAI's dots launch]] distributes persistent agents through existing ChatGPT Pro, Business Premium, and Enterprise plans. The initial allowance holiday and unpublished later usage terms leave the incremental monetization bridge open.
- [[wiki/events/2026-06-18 UK police forces report six months of Bobbi agent operations|The Bobbi deployment]] adds six months of scaled public-service use through Salesforce Agentforce. It strengthens the distribution mechanism without disclosing the contract or Salesforce-attributed revenue.
- [[wiki/events/2026-10-08 Microsoft reports ESS adoption and ticket reduction|Microsoft's ESS deployment]] adds operator evidence that an incumbent can distribute an employee-support agent to more than 300,000 workers, but also shows that installed access did not guarantee adoption until trust, context, and escalation were redesigned.
- [[wiki/events/2026-10-08 The RealReal launches Ask TRR to all members|The RealReal's Ask TRR launch]] places a shopping agent inside a 45 million-member marketplace with proprietary inventory and transaction rails. It closes a production-availability step but provides no active-use, conversion, gross-merchandise-value, or commission-revenue evidence.

## Challenging evidence

- Muse's initial retention advantage was not durable against ChatGPT: ChatGPT overtook it between days eight and nine and reached 26.8% on day fourteen versus Muse's 22.5%. The independent estimates do not disclose the cohort denominator, paid conversion, or acquisition mix.

- Integration announcements and ARR commitments do not yet prove sustained agent usage, better outcomes, or durable platform power; the new Five9 evidence still does not isolate agent-attributed economics.
- Sierra's role at Singtel and R1 shows that a specialist agent vendor can own the orchestration layer even when incumbent systems and customer channels supply distribution.
- C Spire and Rossmann materially strengthen the operating evidence, but both remain vendor-published stories without independent customer-side measurement or audited economics.
- Microsoft's ratio has no absolute agent, run, user, or firm count, and its active definition allows very light use; it supports distribution direction without establishing economic depth.
- Salesforce's agreement is a contract ceiling across a broad software stack, not a measure of exercised, agent-specific revenue or operational adoption.
- Salesforce's Agentforce ARR is company-reported annualized contract value rather than recognized revenue, and its combined AI/data bookings and AWUs do not disclose retention, customer concentration, or agent-specific margin.
- Workday's customer count does not disclose active-agent depth, paid attach, task volume, renewal, outcomes, or agent-specific ACV; broader AI ACV cannot be treated as agent revenue.
- Workday's credits also cover Data Cloud and Sana. Credit purchases and broader AI annual contract value therefore cannot be assigned to agents without a product-level bridge.
- Serval's evidence is vendor-reported and does not establish that the AI-native layer bypasses incumbent systems in customer acquisition, data ownership, permissions, or durable revenue capture.
- [[wiki/events/2026-08-25 Haizhi reports direct Atlas agent revenue and margin growth|Haizhi reports direct Atlas agent revenue and margin growth]] shows a specialist agent and ontology vendor generating direct agent revenue outside the largest software incumbents. Its 69% conversion among existing Atlas Graph customers also suggests that installed-base distribution can accrue to specialist data platforms, not only global systems of record.
- [[wiki/events/2026-08-26 Runable reports rapid paid launch and negative gross margin|Runable's rapid paid launch]] shows a specialist application reaching a reported $2 million annualized revenue run rate without incumbent distribution, but negative gross margin and undisclosed retention materially weaken durability.
- [[wiki/events/2026-08-27 ServiceNow patches three maximum-severity AI Platform flaws|ServiceNow's three CVSS 10.0 AI Platform flaws]] show that concentrating agents, credentials, data, and actions in an incumbent control plane can also concentrate unauthenticated code-execution, privilege, and database risk. No exploitation was known at disclosure.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-03 Amber International reports agentic revenue and margin expansion|Amber International's A-MM and MIA revenue disclosure]] shows a specialist financial and marketing operator monetizing agent-native workflows without relying on a global system-of-record platform. The category boundary and recurrence remain uncertain.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-02 Snowflake links agent adoption to AI revenue acceleration|Snowflake's Q2 FY2027 update]] links 9,100-plus CoCo accounts, 5,800 CoWork accounts, and higher core consumption to a broad AI-revenue step-up. This strengthens the installed-platform distribution thesis, although agent-product revenue remains unseparated.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-04 Agibank scales Agentforce service and orchestration|Agibank's Agentforce deployment]] adds a named regulated customer running six agents and 11 bots through WhatsApp and CRM records at 70,000 monthly inquiries. It shows scaled distribution but does not disclose Salesforce contract economics.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-07 NCSC warns shadow AI can extend agent privileges outside governance|NCSC's shadow-AI guidance]] qualifies the thesis by describing employee adoption through unapproved tools outside formal systems and governance. It is institutional risk guidance rather than measured platform-share evidence.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-10 IBEX reports AI-agent wins and implementations|IBEX's two agent-specific customer wins and more than 10 completed implementations]] show a service operator distributing Sierra-powered agent workflows through established enterprise CX relationships. This weakens the claim that the software system of record alone captures distribution, while the missing contract values and renewal evidence limit the commercial conclusion.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-17 Datasea begins agent marketing services for five customers|Datasea's five service-started customers]] show another specialist marketing-services route to agent deployment, but the issuer's usage-priced pre-orders do not establish recognized revenue, retention, or independent customer economics.
- [[wiki/events/2026-10-13 Datasea reports repeat service-value growth|Datasea's three-week follow-up]] strengthens the specialist counterexample: the same five-customer cohort increased aggregate service value from approximately $1.10 million to $4.0 million. Recognition, collection, retention, and agent-only attribution remain unresolved.
- [[wiki/events/2026-10-05 Vida introduces outcome-based agent billing|Vida's outcome-billing model]] gives an agent-native public company a direct completed-result toll outside a broad enterprise system of record. No outcome-billed customer or realized revenue has yet been disclosed.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-24 Zenity discloses patched Agentforce SalesBleed chains|Zenity's SalesBleed proof of concept]] shows the security inverse of incumbent distribution: public CRM input, default read access, downstream rendering, and a trusted collaboration identity can combine into a wider blast radius. Zenity reports that Salesforce patched the demonstrated chains before publication, and no customer incident or prevalence denominator was disclosed.
- [[wiki/events/2026-09-25 Cognition crosses one billion dollar annualized revenue run rate|Cognition's revenue update]] shows a specialist agent company reaching a reported $1 billion annualized revenue run rate without owning a broad enterprise system of record. Its named customers and Agent Compute Unit growth weaken an incumbent-only distribution claim, while the private-company figures lack product mix, retention, margin, and independent verification.
- [[wiki/events/2026-10-21 Walmart announces Agent Profiles and Gateway|Walmart's Agent Profiles and Gateway announcement]] shows a retailer using its customer accounts, permissions, product data, payment methods, order approval, and returns process to define how outside agents may reach customers and transact. The control surface is clear, but no adoption or economic outcome is live.
- [[wiki/events/2026-10-27 Salesforce reports Agent Fabric request scale and control rollout|Salesforce's Agent Fabric update]] extends an incumbent API and workflow platform into cross-platform agent discovery, evaluation, policy, intervention, and budget control. More than 1.2 billion daily requests indicates scale, but the request mix, customer count, paid attach, and agent-specific economics remain undisclosed.
- [[wiki/events/2026-10-28 ServiceNow raises AI ACV target as agentic consumption grows|ServiceNow's Q3 update]] strengthens the thesis by pairing a raised AI ACV target with more than thirteenfold production agentic-consumption growth inside its workflow platform, although agent-specific ACV is not separated.
- [[wiki/events/2026-10-28 CargoAi reports scaled production agent activity|CargoAi's scaled air-cargo activity]] qualifies the thesis: a specialist vertical platform can become the distribution and execution surface by controlling live rates, quotes, booking, tracking, and operator integrations.
- [[wiki/events/2026-10-29 AWS reports AgentCore customer and deployment growth|AWS AgentCore's Q3 adoption]] strengthens the thesis at the cloud-control-plane layer. Tens of thousands of customers and deployment growth show that existing cloud accounts, billing, compute, identity, policy, and observability can distribute agent infrastructure, although paid attach, workload depth, and retention are not disclosed.
- [[wiki/events/2026-10-30 Supabase reports agent-linked database and revenue growth|Supabase's database and revenue growth]] strengthens the thesis at the data-infrastructure layer. AI tools create more than 60% of new databases, and the platform now provisions more than one million databases each week. The company-level revenue increase is material, but agent-attributed revenue, paid conversion, retention, and margin remain unseparated.

## What would change the thesis

- Named scaled deployments and workflow-completion metrics would strengthen it.
- Strong direct-to-agent platforms that bypass enterprise incumbents would weaken it.
