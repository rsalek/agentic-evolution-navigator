# ClickHouse

A private real-time analytics and observability company. Its open-source database is complemented by the paid, managed ClickHouse Cloud service, Managed ClickStack, Langfuse Cloud, and ClickHouse Agents. The commercial exposure spans high-volume agent application data, agent-behaviour observability, model-cost tracking, and agent-driven analytics.
