# Transportation Security Administration

A United States federal transportation-security agency operating the Ace traveler-support agent through Salesforce Public Sector Solutions and Government Cloud.

Salesforce's named-customer disclosure reports that Ace handles approximately 100,000 routine traveler conversations per month, resolves 96% without human escalation, and has reduced interaction cost by more than 90% from a baseline above $4 per conversation.
