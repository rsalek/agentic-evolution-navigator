# Snowflake

An AI Data Cloud provider positioning Cortex AI Gateway as a control plane for governed first-party and third-party agents.

In Q2 FY2027, Snowflake reported 9,100-plus CoCo accounts, including more than 2,000 net additions in the quarter, and 5,800 CoWork accounts, up nearly 11% quarter over quarter. Management explicitly linked CoWork consumption and CoCo adoption to a meaningful increase in AI revenue and higher core-platform consumption, while declining to isolate agent-product revenue.

Snowflake announced Agent Observability for private preview on September 22. The product adds agent tracing, evaluation, cost measurement, and links to business outcomes, but Snowflake disclosed no customer, pricing, paid attach, usage, or revenue evidence.
