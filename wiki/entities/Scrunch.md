# Scrunch

An AI customer-experience and agent-facing website-delivery platform. Scrunch reports $26 million of funding and 500-plus customers, and its Agent Experience Platform creates and serves a parallel AI-ready version of customer pages.

Scrunch's official company page says it became a Sitecore company in 2026. It is therefore a strategic acquisition outcome, not an independently investable startup opportunity unless Sitecore creates a separate instrument.
