# Haizhi Technology

Beijing Haizhi Technology Group is a Hong Kong-listed enterprise data and agent-platform company (HKEX: 2706). Its Atlas portfolio combines graph and multimodal databases, business ontology, and an agent harness for governed execution in enterprise and industrial workflows.
