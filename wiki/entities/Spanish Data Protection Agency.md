# Spanish Data Protection Agency

Spain's data-protection regulator, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD). On September 14, 2026 it disclosed receiving a personal-data-breach notification alleging that an AI agent helped chain attack steps. The regulator explicitly said the notification still required analysis.
