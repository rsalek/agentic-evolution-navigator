# HUMAN Security

A cybersecurity and traffic-governance provider whose Human Defense Platform classifies human, bot, crawler, and AI-agent activity. Its monthly traffic reports provide first-party telemetry from the platform, but do not establish market-wide traffic shares or agent-specific product economics.
