# Why public AI usage studies disagree

Public AI-usage studies often report different task, automation, and adoption rates because they observe different products, populations, units, thresholds, and definitions. The disagreement is frequently methodological rather than factual.

## Verified comparison

- [Google ATLAS](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/research/understanding-the-ai-economy/) covers 15 million interactions across the Gemini App, AI Mode, and Gemini API. It is unusually broad geographically but excludes major workplace, enterprise, and agentic surfaces.
- [OpenAI B2B Signals](https://openai.com/signals/b2b/) compares the 95th-percentile firm with the median firm. Its 16x Codex-messages-per-worker gap measures relative depth among business users, not an adoption rate or outcome.
- [Microsoft's 2026 Work Trend Index](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/agents-human-agency-and-the-opportunity-for-every-organization) counts unique active agent objects across Microsoft 365 and SharePoint agents. The 15x growth rate has a defined period but no absolute count.
- [Anthropic's September 2025 Economic Index](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-september-2025-report) reports a rise in directive Claude.ai conversations from 27% to 39%. It is consumer telemetry, and a classifier change complicates the direct time comparison.
- [Federal Reserve-linked survey research](https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/publications/system-research-st-louis-fed/2026/07/what-work-does-generative-ai-do/) finds generative-AI use in 40% of job tasks. The authors explicitly say worker surveys and platform chat logs produce systematically different task shares.

## Corrections to the discovery packet

The user-supplied comparison PDF is useful discovery material, not proof. Primary review found two important precision issues:

- Google's less-than-10% automation figure applies to non-routine cognitive-work conversations, not all work interactions.
- The PDF's citation for Anthropic's 27%-to-39% directive shift points to the 2025 launch page, while the supporting comparison appears in the September 2025 Economic Index report.

## Durable interpretation

The defensible cross-study pattern is broad mainstream assistance plus deeper delegation on enterprise and agentic surfaces. The figures should not be averaged or ranked as if they share a denominator. Each claim should preserve its measurement surface and be tested against absolute task volume, accepted outcomes, correction, control, and economics.
