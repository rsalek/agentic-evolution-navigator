    How to read this

    The orange line represents demand placed on publishers. The green line represents attributable referral value returned. A wider distance is a warning signal, not proof of economic loss.

    1

    What we observe

    Identified agent demand expands while attributable referral value weakens across the selected period.

    • Traffic is observable and increasing.
    • Access controls increasingly distinguish agent requests.
    • The values remain illustrative until connected to dated Radar snapshots.
    2

    What it may mean

    The web may be becoming easier for agents to consume before the value exchange becomes reciprocal.

    • This supports the addressable-market thesis.
    • It challenges the assumption that more traffic automatically creates publisher value.
    • Readiness alone does not establish adoption or willingness to pay.
    3

    What would confirm or change the thesis

    Commercial evidence must close the gap between technical demand and economic value.

    • Verified conversion or attributable revenue.
    • Paid access or licensing at repeatable scale.
    • Evidence that agents create net-new demand or user benefit.

    Keep this in Signals until the change is durable.

    A new standard, major operator shift, or sustained multi-period inflection must be verified before this becomes a dated graph event.
